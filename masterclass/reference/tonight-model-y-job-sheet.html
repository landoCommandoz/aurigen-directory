Exterior + One-Step Polish

One bucket, towel-per-panel, garden hose. Clay in the wash. Polish in the garage after dark. Ceramic wax tomorrow morning.

Why 4 oz
Label is 1 oz per 5 gal to wash, 1 oz per gal to strip wax. You are polishing tonight. Strip it.
Towel rule
One towel per panel, folded in quarters. Lift it out dripping, squeeze it once over the top edge so soap runs ahead of you, then pull it straight back. Then the dirty pile, never back in the bucket.
Short on towels
Upper panels can share one towel on a fresh face each panel. Bumpers, rockers and the bottom of the doors never share.
Hose
No pressure washer, so no foam soak. Nozzle on jet, 3 to 4 min top down before any towel touches paint, extra on bumpers, rockers and behind the wheels. Last rinse: nozzle off, open hose, flood it top down so it sheets off.
Clay tub
Separate tub: 1 gal water + 1 oz Citrus. Mitt lives here. Hose-rinse the mitt after every panel.
IPA bottle
Zep sprayer: 1 cup 70% isopropyl + 3 cups water (distilled if you have it).
Wheels
No bucket. Armor All Extreme Wheel & Tire, brush, hose.
Not in it
Ultimate Wash & Wax, dish soap, Spic and Span, wheel brushes, the clay mitt.
