    note: Tire completely dry, spray the applicator not the tire, thin even coat, wipe the rubber-to-wheel edge, let it set a few minutes before the car moves. A thick coat slings black spots on the first drive. Nothing slick before the paint is polished. No tire shine on a motorcycle.
stopIf:
  - No tire shine on a motorcycle. Ever. Not the sidewall, not near it.
  - Nothing slick goes on the car until the paint is polished.
checks:
  - Was the tire completely dry?
  - Did you spray the applicator or the tire?
  - Is the coat thin or thick?
  - Did you wipe the rubber-to-wheel edge?
  - Did the car move before it set?
causes:
  - A thick coat. It slings black spots down the side on the first drive.
  - Sprayed on the tire, not on the applicator.
  - The tire was not completely dry.
  - The rubber-to-wheel edge was not wiped.
  - The car moved before the shine set.
steps:
  - Make sure the tire is completely dry.
  - Spray the tire shine onto the applicator, not the tire.
  - Put on a thin, even coat.
  - Wipe the rubber-to-wheel edge.
  - Let it set a few minutes before the car moves.
