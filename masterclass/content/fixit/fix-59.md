checks:
  - Is the light held low across the paint, not pointed straight at it?
  - Did you also look from a few feet back?
  - After polishing, did you IPA wipe first?
causes:
  - The light is pointed straight at the paint instead of low across it.
  - Polish oils hide swirls until you IPA wipe.
steps:
  - Hold the light low across the paint.
  - Look for a spiderweb pattern. Those are swirls.
  - Step back a few feet and look again.
  - Fingernail-test any scratch you find.
  - Photograph every flaw before you start.
