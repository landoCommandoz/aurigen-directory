id: fix-70
type: fixnode
title: Steering wheel or pedals feel slick
group: interior
audience: [owner, crew]
visibility: internal
status: house
kbRef: "10"
tags: [interior, steering wheel, pedals, slick, safety]
sayIt:
  - steering wheel is slippery
  - slick steering wheel
  - pedals are slick
  - slippery pedals
  - greasy steering wheel
  - wheel feels oily
  - foot slips on the pedal
  - got dressing on the pedals
  - shiny steering wheel
  - hands slide on the wheel
sources:
  - label: Knowledge base 10 and 21
    date: 2026-10-07
    note: Steering wheel and pedals end clean, dry, and with zero slick. Spray the towel, never the panel. FIX-32 gives the clean damp towel, then dry, for too much product on the dash.
stopIf:
  - The steering wheel and pedals must have zero slick before the car goes back.
checks:
  - Rub the wheel and each pedal with a dry hand. Any slick at all?
  - Did any dressing or spray land on them?
causes:
  - Dressing or overspray landed on the wheel or the pedals.
  - Product sprayed on the panel, not the towel.
steps:
  - text: Wipe the wheel and the pedals with a clean, damp towel.
    status: verify
  - text: Dry them with a clean, dry towel.
    status: verify
  - Check them again with a dry hand.
  - Repeat until there is zero slick.
stillHappening:
  - text: Still slick after the wipe. The knowledge base has no next step yet.
    status: verify
notes:
  - text: The damp-then-dry wipe is the knowledge base fix for too much product on the dash. For the wheel and pedals it is not confirmed.
    status: verify
  - Dress plastics matte. Nothing goes on the wheel or pedals that leaves them slick.
