checks:
  - Did the final rinse sheet off with the nozzle off?
  - Did the compressor hit the gaps before the towels?
  - Are the doors open and the jambs dried?
causes:
  - No sheeting rinse, so too much water stayed on the car.
  - The compressor skipped the gaps, so they drip later.
  - Towels rubbed around instead of laid flat and pulled.
  - The jambs were not opened and dried.
steps:
  - Take the nozzle off the hose.
  - Let the water sheet off, top down, with the hose open.
  - "Blow out the gaps with the compressor first: mirrors, handles, badges, charge port door, liftgate seams, grille, trim seams."
  - Lay a towel flat on the panel.
  - Pull it toward you.
  - Open every door.
  - Dry the jambs.
  - Hit any drip with the compressor again.
