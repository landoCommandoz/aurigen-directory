    date: 2026-10-07
    note: Shade, cool panels, no wind. Too hot to keep your hand on, stop and let it cool. Plastic bumpers heat fast. Never park the machine spinning in one spot.
stopIf:
  - Too hot to keep your hand on. Stop and let it cool.
  - Sun on the panel. Do not polish in the sun.
checks:
  - Put your hand flat on the panel. Can you keep it there?
  - Is the panel in the sun?
  - Is it a plastic bumper?
  - Has the pad sat in one spot?
causes:
  - Working in the sun, not the shade.
  - Plastic bumpers heat fast.
  - Too much pressure, too high a speed, or too long in one spot.
steps:
  - Let go of the trigger with the pad still on the paint.
  - Lift the pad.
  - Let the panel cool.
  - Move the car or the work into shade.
  - "On a plastic bumper: lighter pressure, lower speed, shorter time."
  - Keep the machine moving. Never park it spinning in one spot.
  - Do the hand test again before you start.
