checks:
  - Unplug it first.
  - text: Hooks on the plate bald or melted?
    status: sourced
  - Back of the pad worn?
  - Is the plate or the pad hot?
  - text: Too much product on the pad?
    status: sourced
  - text: Pad back the same size as the plate? Measure the hook-and-loop back, not the face.
    status: verify
causes:
  - Worn hooks on the plate or the pad.
  - Heat.
  - text: Too much product. The 56367 manual says a pad that keeps coming off may have too much wax on it.
    status: sourced
  - text: A pad back that does not match the plate size.
    status: verify
steps:
  - Unplug the polisher.
  - Look at the hooks on the plate.
  - Look at the back of the pad.
  - "Pad back worn: replace the pad."
  - "Plate hooks bald or melted: replace the plate. Open the backing plate fix."
  - Let the plate and pad cool.
  - Use less product. After priming, 1 to 2 drops per section.
  - Center the pad on the plate before you start again.
stillHappening:
  - "New pad still won't hold: the plate hooks are the problem. Open the backing plate fix."
notes:
  - Swap pads when loaded or hot, roughly every two panels.
