checks:
  - Are you wiping and re-applying between passes?
  - Are you putting a full dose on every section?
  - How many sets do you run before you wipe?
causes:
  - Wiping and re-applying between passes.
  - A full dose on every section after the pad is primed.
steps:
  - Prime a new pad once.
  - After that, use 1 to 2 drops per section.
  - Run 4 to 6 sets on the square.
  - Wipe once, at the end of the square's sets.
  - Never wipe and re-apply between passes.
