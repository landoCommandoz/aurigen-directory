    note: Order is everything out, mats, knock it down from the top (headliner, vents, seams, seat rails), then vacuum top down (seats, under seats, carpet, trunk; slide seats forward, then back). Cup holders and seams get the Dremel Versa bristle pad, then the vacuum. Vacuum takes 25 minutes in the plan.
checks:
  - Did you knock it down from the top before you vacuumed?
  - Did you vacuum top down?
  - Did you slide the seats forward, then back?
  - Did the cup holders and seams get the bristle pad?
causes:
  - Vacuumed before knocking dust down from the top.
  - Vacuumed the carpet before the seats.
  - Seats not slid, so the space under them was missed.
  - Cup holders and seams vacuumed without the bristle pad first.
steps:
  - "Knock it down from the top: headliner, vents, seams, seat rails."
  - Clean the cup holders and seams with the Dremel Versa bristle pad.
  - Vacuum the seats first.
  - Slide the seats forward.
  - Vacuum under and behind them.
  - Slide the seats back.
  - Vacuum the space in front of them.
  - Vacuum the carpet.
  - Vacuum the trunk last.
