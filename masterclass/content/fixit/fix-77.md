  - A compound pad never touches polish. One pad per liquid.

  - Wheel towels never rejoin the paint pile.

  - How many clean towels are left, and for which job?
  - How many clean, fully dry pads are left?
  - Were the towels counted into piles the night before?
causes:
  - Towels and pads were not counted the night before.
  - Two cars booked without doubling the pad count.
  - Pads from the last job were not washed the same night, or are not dry yet.
steps:
  - Count what is left.
  - Save the clean towels for the jobs still ahead.
  - Do not grab a towel from another pile.
  - Blow out the pad you are using after every square.
  - Tonight, count the towels into piles for the next car.
  - Wash every used pad the same night.
  - Before two cars in a weekend, double the pad count.
