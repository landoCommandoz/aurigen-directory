checks:
  - Press a towel on the carpet. Does it come up wet?
  - Are the mats standing in the sun?
  - Are the windows cracked or the fan running?
causes:
  - The mats were washed late in the job.
  - No air on the wet carpet.
  - The mats were laid flat, not stood in the sun.
steps:
  - Crack the windows or run the fan.
  - Stand the mats up in the sun.
  - Next time, soak the mats while you do the wheels.
