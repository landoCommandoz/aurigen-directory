sources:
  - label: Knowledge base 8.3 and 8.7
    date: 2026-10-07
    note: Crosshatch, one set left to right overlapping by half then top to bottom, 4 to 6 sets per square. Creamy, then see-through, then almost gone; clear means spent, stop. Last set floats. Wipe once at the end. IPA, wipe, light low and from a few feet back. About 4 minutes per 2 x 2 ft section on a one-step.
stopIf:
  - Paint color on the pad. STOP. You are through the clear. Old single-stage paint is the only exception.
checks:
  - "Watch the polish: creamy, see-through, or almost gone?"
  - How many sets have you run on this square?
  - How long have you been on this square?
causes:
  - Sets not counted. One square gets 4 to 6 sets.
  - Working past the point where the polish goes clear. Working too long makes it dust.
steps:
  - "Count each set: left to right overlapping by half, then top to bottom."
  - Run 4 to 6 sets.
  - "Watch the polish: creamy, then see-through, then almost gone."
  - When it goes clear, it is spent. Stop.
  - On the last set, let the machine float on its own weight.
  - Let go of the trigger with the pad still on the paint.
  - Lift the pad.
  - Wipe the square once.
  - Mist it with IPA and wipe with a clean towel.
  - Check it with the light low, then from a few feet back.
stillHappening:
  - "Swirls still there after the check: open the swirls still there fix."
  - "Polish turning to powder: open the dusting fix."
notes:
  - About 4 minutes per 2 x 2 ft section on a one-step, including the wipe and the check.
