    date: 2026-10-07
    note: Three piles that never mix. Product towels soak 1 hour in warm soapy water first; wax-residue towels get at least two washes. Hot water for the product piles (sourced 2026-10-07; Meguiar's washes its own polishing cloths hot), warm for the water-only pile. Dye-free, fragrance-free detergent, full cycle, extra rinse. Never fabric softener, dryer sheets, or bleach. Microfiber only. Dry on low or air dry; dryer heat melts microfiber.
stopIf:
  - Never fabric softener, dryer sheets, or bleach.
  - Never a hot dryer. Dryer heat melts microfiber.
checks:
  - Did fabric softener or dryer sheets touch them?
  - Was the dryer on low?
  - Were they washed with cotton or terry?
  - Did the product towels soak first?
causes:
  - Fabric softener or dryer sheets. Check the softener cup on a shared washer.
  - The dryer was too hot.
  - Washed with cotton or terry.
  - Product towels did not soak first, or wax towels got only one wash.
  - Dyed or scented detergent.
steps:
  - Sort the towels into three piles. Water-only, product, and wheels.
  - Soak the product pile 1 hour in warm soapy water.
  - Empty the softener cup if the washer is shared.
  - Use dye-free, fragrance-free detergent.
  - text: Wash the product piles in hot water.
    status: sourced
  - Wash the water-only pile in warm water.
  - Run a full cycle with an extra rinse.
  - Wash wax-residue towels at least twice.
  - Dry on low, or air dry.
stillHappening:
  - text: Still stiff or streaky after a proper wash. Whether the towel can be saved is not in the knowledge base.
    status: verify
notes:
  - Microfiber only. Never with cotton or terry.
  - The wheel pile is its own load, and those towels stay wheel towels for good.
  - Hot water in a home washer does not hurt microfiber. Dryer heat does.
