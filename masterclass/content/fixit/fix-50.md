sources:

    date: 2026-10-07
    note: Wash pads the same night, before polish dries in. Dry face down on a rack. Fully dry before the next use. Dried compound in a pad is a dead pad. More Uro-Tec pads, double the pad count before booking two cars in a weekend.
  - label: Buff and Shine FAQ
    url: https://buffandshine.com/pages/faqs
    date: 2026-10-11
    note: Wash foam pads with a very mild solution of all-purpose cleaner, or on the gentle cycle in warm or cold water, never hot. Air-dry the washed pads on a flat surface. Never put pads in the dryer; they will warp. Never place a pad face down on any surface. Loaded directly. Fact-checker FC-70.
    confirmedOn: 2026-10-11
    confirmedBy: fact-checker
  - label: Buff and Shine buffing tips
    url: https://buffandshine.com/pages/buffing-tips
    date: 2026-10-11
    note: Soak foam pads in warm water and wring dry. Never put them in the dryer. Loaded directly. Fact-checker FC-70.
    confirmedOn: 2026-10-11
    confirmedBy: fact-checker
