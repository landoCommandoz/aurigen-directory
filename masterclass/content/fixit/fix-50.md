checks:
  - Was the pad washed the same night it was used?
  - Is it fully dry?
  - Is the face crusty or hard?
causes:
  - Polish or compound dried in because the pad was not washed the same night.
  - The pad was washed but is not fully dry yet.
steps:
  - Take that pad off.
  - Put on a clean, fully dry pad.
  - "Damp pad: dry it face down on a rack before its next use."
  - "Crusty compound pad: it is a dead pad. Replace it."
  - Wash every used pad the same night from now on.
