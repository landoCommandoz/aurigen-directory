stopIf:
  - No tire shine on a motorcycle. Ever. Not the sidewall, not near it.
  - text: Armor All's tire shine label says not for cycle tires, cycle seats, pedals, grips, or steering wheels.
    status: sourced
  - Nothing slick on brake rotors, grips, pegs, or the seat.
  - Bike not stone cold? Wait. Water on hot pipes stains chrome.
  - text: "Windshield: no alcohol, no glass cleaner, no compound. It is plastic."
    status: sourced
  - "No clay on the windshield either."
  - text: Wheel cleaner on a bike? The Armor All label says check with the bike maker first. Keep it off the brakes.
    status: sourced

    status: verify
checks:
  - Is the bike stone cold?
  - Is the hose on low?
  - Is the paint matte, like Harley Denim?
  - What product is in your hand, and where is it going?
causes:
  - A bike has parts a car does not have, like rotors, grips, pegs, a seat, a chain, and a plastic windshield. Car habits hurt them.
steps:
  - Wait until the bike is stone cold.
  - Set the hose on low.
  - Keep water pressure off the chain, wheel bearings, electrics, under the seat, and the air intake.
  - Dry it with the air compressor, every crevice.
  - Keep tire shine away from the bike.
  - Keep anything slick off the rotors, grips, pegs, and seat.
  - Hand-polish the tank and fenders.
  - Polish the chrome with chrome polish.
  - text: Give the chrome a coat of ceramic wax.
    status: verify
  - text: Clean the chain with an O-ring safe chain cleaner only.
    status: sourced
  - text: Lube the chain with an O-ring safe chain lube only.
    status: sourced
