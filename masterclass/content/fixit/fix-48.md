    note: Two towels, one wet, one dry, never one that touched wax or polish. Drop windows 1 to 2 inches and clean the top edge. Windshield from the passenger seat for the far corner. Glass is last. On the Model Y, camera lenses get glass cleaner only.
checks:
  - Did you drop the window 1 to 2 inches?
  - Did you do the windshield's far corner from the passenger seat?
  - Was the glass done last?
causes:
  - The window was not dropped, so the top edge got missed.
  - The far corner of the windshield is hard to reach from the driver's side.
  - Glass was done before other work, so it got dirty again.
steps:
  - Drop the window 1 to 2 inches.
  - Clean the top edge.
  - Sit in the passenger seat.
  - Clean the windshield's far corner from there.
  - Use two towels, one wet and one dry.
  - Do glass last, last thing inside and last thing outside.
stillHappening:
  - "Streaks on the glass: open the glass streaks fix."
notes:
  - Never use a towel that touched wax or polish on glass.
  - text: What wets the glass towel, water or a glass cleaner, is not set in the knowledge base yet.
    status: verify
  - On a Tesla, camera lenses get glass cleaner only.
