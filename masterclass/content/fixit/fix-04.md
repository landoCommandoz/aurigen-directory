checks:
  - "New pad: was it primed?"

  - Is the square bigger than 2 x 2 ft?

causes:
  - Pad not primed.
  - Pad dried out.
  - Section too big.
  - Panel hot or in the sun.
steps:
  - Mist the pad face lightly with water, from a spray bottle or a damp hand.
  - Do not soak it.
  - Add 1 to 2 drops of polish.
  - Keep the square 2 x 2 ft.
  - Move the car or the work into shade.
  - Let the panel cool until you can hold your hand on it.
  - Re-mist every 2 or 3 sections as needed.
