  - Dry pad.
  - Too much heat.
steps:
  - Work shorter cycles.
  - "Watch the polish: creamy, then see-through, then almost gone."
  - When it goes clear, it is spent. Stop.
  - Mist the pad face lightly with water.
  - Do not soak it.
  - Use less product, 1 to 2 drops.
  - Check the heat with your hand on the panel.
