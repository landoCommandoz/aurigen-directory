checks:
  - Is everything on one cart or bin set at the back corner of the car?
  - Is anything more than three steps away?
  - Was the kit pre-staged the night before?
  - Is the setup timer running?
causes:
  - Nothing was pre-staged the night before.
  - Gear is spread around the garage, not on one cart.
  - The bins are not sorted by stage.
  - The bucket is not mixed, the towels are not counted, or the pads are not dry.
steps:
  - Put everything on one cart or bin set.
  - Park it at the back corner of the car.
  - Keep everything within three steps.
  - "Sort the bins by stage: Wheels, Wash, Clay, Polish, Protect, Interior, Glass, Cleanup."
  - Start the setup timer.
  - The night before, mix or measure the bucket.
  - Count the towels into piles.
  - Check the pads are clean and dry.
  - Fill the IPA bottle and label it.
  - Charge the compressor.
  - Run the cords.
  - Put the tape in your pocket.
