  - Is the pad flat on the paint before you pull the trigger?
  - Do you let go of the trigger before you lift?
  - What speed are you spreading on?
  - How much product is on the pad?
causes:
  - Too much product.
  - Started or stopped off the paint.
  - Spreading at high speed.
steps:
  - Use 1 to 2 drops per section after priming.
  - Set the pad flat on the paint before you pull the trigger.
  - Spread on speed 1 to 2 across the whole square.
  - Let go of the trigger with the pad still on the paint.
  - Lift the pad after it stops.
