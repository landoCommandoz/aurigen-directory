sources:
  - label: Knowledge base 2, rule 9, 7.3, 8.1, and 8.3
    date: 2026-10-07
    note: Stay an inch off edges and body lines. Tape them. Edges and body lines always read low. Curves use the middle of the pad; never tilt onto an edge.
stopIf:
  - Paint color on the pad. STOP. You are through the clear. Old single-stage paint is the only exception.
  - Never run the pad over an edge or a body line. Stay an inch off.
checks:
  - Are the edges and sharp body lines taped?
  - Is the pad an inch off the edge?
  - On a curve, is the pad tilted onto its edge?
causes:
  - Edges and body lines were not taped.
  - The pad tilted onto its edge on a curve.
  - Edges and body lines always read low on the gauge.
steps:
  - Let go of the trigger with the pad still on the paint.
  - Lift the pad.
  - Tape the sharp body lines and the panel edges.
  - Keep the pad an inch off edges and body lines.
  - On a curve, use the middle of the pad.
  - Never tilt the pad onto its edge.
stillHappening:
  - "Color on the pad by an edge: open the paint color on the pad fix."
notes:
  - "On paint protection film: never polish over a film edge."
  - When you measure, stay an inch off edges too. They always read low.
