sayIt:
  - wrong pad
  - grabbed the wrong pad
  - pads mixed up
  - mixed up the pads
  - used the compound pad for polish
  - polish on the compound pad
  - compound on the polish pad
  - same pad for both
  - which pad was which
  - one pad for compound and polish
sources:
  - label: Knowledge base 8.2 and 8.5
    date: 2026-10-07
    note: One pad per liquid. A compound pad never touches polish. Wipe compound off with its own towel. Every compounded square gets the yellow and M210 pass.
checks:
  - Which pad touched which liquid?
  - After a wipe and IPA, is the square hazy under the light?
causes:
  - One pad was used for two liquids.
steps:
  - Let go of the trigger with the pad still on the paint.
  - Lift the pad.
  - Take that pad off the plate.
  - Set it aside for cleanup tonight.
  - Put on a clean pad for the liquid you are using.
  - Wipe the square once.
  - Mist it with IPA and wipe with a clean towel.
  - Check it with the light held low.
  - text: Haze on that square? Follow it with the yellow pad and M210, the same as a compounded square.
    status: verify
stillHappening:
  - Keep one pad per liquid for the rest of the car.
notes:
  - One pad per liquid. A compound pad never touches polish.
  - Wipe compound off with its own towel.
