## What a right answer sounds like

- Clear coat: the clear top layer, and the only layer you polish.
- Base coat: the color under the clear.
- Single-stage paint: old paint where the color is the top layer, so some color on the pad is normal. Say it, note it, back off pressure.
- Microns: how paint thickness is measured. The gauge reads µm. 1 mil is 25.4 µm.

## Why

If you can explain it, you can say it to a customer standing at the car.
