## Why

Polish that dries into a pad kills it. That is why pads get washed the same night.

Buff and Shine makes the Uro-Tec pads, and it agrees with the house on warm water, never hot. It adds a hard rule: never in the dryer, because heat warps the foam. It disagrees on drying: the house dries pads face down on a rack, and Buff and Shine says dry them flat and never face down. Lando decides which way the shop does it.
