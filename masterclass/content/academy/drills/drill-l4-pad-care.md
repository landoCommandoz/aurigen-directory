needs:
  - Used Uro-Tec pads from a practice session
  - The air compressor
  - Warm water and laundry detergent
  - A drying rack
instructions:
  - During the session, blow out or brush the pad after every square.
  - The same night, pull the pad off the backing plate.
  - Wash it in warm water, not hot, with a drop of laundry detergent.
  - Work the face until the water runs clear.
  - Press the water out. Never wring it.
  - Dry it face down on a rack.
  - Wipe the polish off the backing plate.
