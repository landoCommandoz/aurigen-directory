## Why

This drill turns the safety lesson into a habit you do with your hands. Lando watches the first few times.

Three new lines come straight from labels and the polisher's manual. The wheel cleaner label says wear eye protection and rubber gloves. Meguiar's safety sheet says the Hot Shine aerosol can is flammable and may burst if heated, and this shop has a heat gun. The manual says a cord used outdoors is marked W-A, a damp place gets a GFCI supply, and plug connections stay off the ground.

The lines that are not confirmed yet stay on the list as pending. You see what is still missing instead of guessing. Who may run the polisher and the other power tools is waiting on Lando.
