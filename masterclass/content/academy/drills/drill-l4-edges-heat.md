  - Keep the pad an inch off every edge and body line.
  - On the curve, use the middle of the pad. Never tilt onto an edge.
  - After every set, put your hand on the panel.
  - Too hot to keep your hand on? Stop and let it cool.
  - On a plastic bumper, use lighter pressure, a lower speed, and a shorter time.
  - Pull the tape and check the trim.
