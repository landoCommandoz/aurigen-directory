needs:
  - A car that is not a Tesla (see the Tesla rows)
  - Armor All Extreme Wheel & Tire
  - The soft, long, and stiff wheel brushes
  - The hose
  - Wheel towels
  - A timer
instructions:
  - Start the timer.
  - Put your hand on the first wheel. It must be cool.
  - Spray the cleaner on the face and the barrel.
  - Wait about a minute.
  - Brush the face with the soft brush.
  - Brush the barrel with the long brush.
  - Brush the tire with the stiff brush.
  - Rinse before the cleaner dries.
  - Do the next wheel. One wheel at a time.
  - Keep every brush off the paint and out of the wash bucket.
  - Put every towel that touched a wheel in the wheel pile.
  - Stop the timer after the fourth wheel.
  - text: "On a Tesla, the house wheel step is the same: Armor All Extreme Wheel & Tire, a brush, and the hose."
