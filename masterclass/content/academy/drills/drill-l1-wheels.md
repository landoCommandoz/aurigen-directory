## Why

The wheel cleaner label is the rule here, and it changed two things. The wait is 30 seconds, not a minute. And the label asks for eye protection, rubber gloves, a cool wet wheel, and a test on a hidden spot first.

The timer keeps you honest. Spray all four at once and the last one dries before you reach it.
