needs:
  - Two clean glass towels, one wet and one dry, that never touched wax or polish
  - The glass cleaner Lando uses (not named in the knowledge base yet)
  - A timer
instructions:
  - Do the glass last.
  - Start the timer.
  - Inside, wipe side to side.
  - Drop each window 1 to 2 inches and clean the top edge.
  - Do the far corner of the windshield from the passenger seat.
  - Stop the timer when the inside glass is done.
  - Outside, wipe up and down.
  - Lando looks for streaks from inside and outside.
  - If he finds one, say which side it is on from its direction.
