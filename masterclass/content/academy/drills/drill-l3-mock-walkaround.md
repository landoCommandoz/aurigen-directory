needs:
  - DJ, Suzie, or Lando playing the customer
  - A car
  - The light, the gauge, a sandwich bag, and a phone camera
  - A job sheet
  - A timer
instructions:
  - Start the timer.
  - Walk the car with the customer standing there.
  - Do the bag test with the customer's own hand.
  - Walk the car with the light held low.
  - Measure with the gauge. Door jamb first.
  - Run the 8 checks.
  - Point out every repaint and every deep scratch. Fingernail test with the customer.
  - Photograph every flaw.
  - Ask if the before and after photos may be posted, plates blurred.
  - Read the script out loud, word for word.
  - Get the acknowledgment signed.
  - Stop the timer.
