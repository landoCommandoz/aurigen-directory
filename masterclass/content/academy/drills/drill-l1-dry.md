needs:
  - The hose with the nozzle off
  - The air compressor
  - 2 drying towels
  - A light held low
  - Shade
instructions:
  - Work in shade, on cool panels.
  - Take the nozzle off for the final rinse.
  - Let the open hose sheet the water off, top down.
  - "Blow out the gaps with the compressor: mirrors, handles, badges, charge port door, liftgate seams, grille, and trim seams."
  - Lay a drying towel flat on the panel and pull it toward you.
  - Dry panel by panel, right away.
  - Open every door and dry the jambs.
  - Never walk away from the wet car.
  - Getting polished? No ceramic spray as a drying aid. Nothing slick before the polish.
  - Lando checks every panel with the light held low.
