needs:

  - A primed yellow pad and M210

  - Clean towels
  - A light held low

instructions:
  - text: "Who may run power tools, the polisher included: Lando decides (pending)."
    status: verify
  - Tape a 2 x 2 ft square.
  - Start the timer.
  - Put 1 to 2 drops on the primed pad.
  - Set the pad flat on the paint before you pull the trigger.
  - Spread at speed 1 to 2 across the whole square.
  - text: "House settings: work at speed 4, with about 10 lb of pressure."
    status: house
  - text: The 56367 manual says only use slower speeds for polishing.
    status: sourced
  - text: The 56367 manual says keep pressure off the tool. The foam pad should lightly touch the paint.
    status: sourced
  - text: Lando decides which applies (pending). Until he decides, the house settings stay.
    status: verify
  - Move about 1 inch per second.
  - Run 4 to 6 sets, with the marker line turning.
  - Stop when the polish goes clear.
  - Let the machine float on its own weight on the last set.
  - Let go of the trigger with the pad still on the paint, then lift.
  - Wipe once.
  - Mist with the IPA panel wipe, and wipe with a clean towel.
  - Check with the light low, then from a few feet back.
  - Stop the timer.
  - Blow out the pad. Do the next square.
