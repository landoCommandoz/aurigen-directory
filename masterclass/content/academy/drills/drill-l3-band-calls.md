## Lando's answer key

1. 132: full ladder permitted. Compound is allowed, not required.
2. 100: full ladder permitted.
3. 99: polish only, yellow or maroon + M210. No compound.
4. 90: polish only.
5. 89: tell the customer. Yellow + M210, light pressure, fewest passes.
6. 85: same as 89.
7. 84: tell the customer. Finishing polish only, minimal passes, or hand work.
8. 75: same as 84.
9. 74: no machine. Hand protection only.
10. 235 when the rest of the car reads about 130: repaint. Go gentle or skip. Log it and tell the customer.
11. 98 when the median of the other panels is 140: thin panel, more than 40 µm below. Go light or skip. If you go at all, polish only.
12. 78 taken on a body line when the flat of the panel reads 125: not a real call. Edges always read low. Re-read an inch off the line.

Readings under 90, under 75, or way above the rest: the customer hears before the pad touches paint.
