needs:
  - The shop vac with attachments
  - The Dremel Versa with the bristle pad
  - A timer
  - Lando to check with a light
instructions:
  - Start the timer.
  - "Knock it down from the top: headliner, vents, seams, seat rails."
  - Run the Dremel Versa bristle pad in the cup holders and seams.
  - Vacuum the seats.
  - Slide the seats forward and vacuum under and behind them.
  - Slide the seats back and vacuum again.
  - Vacuum the carpet.
  - Vacuum the trunk.
  - Stop the timer.
  - Lando checks under and between the seats, the seams, and the cup holders.
