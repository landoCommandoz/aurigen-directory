needs:
  - 16 microfiber towels folded in quarters
  - The bucket, mixed for the job
  - The hose
  - A timer
  - Lando watching for rule breaks
instructions:
  - "Mix the bucket for the job. Before a polish: 4 gal water + 4 oz CG Citrus Wash & Gloss."
  - text: "On a maintenance wash: a Meguiar's soap at about 1 oz per gallon."
    status: sourced
  - Put all 16 towels in the bucket.
  - Start the timer.
  - Wash roof to rockers, one panel at a time.
  - Lift a towel out dripping and squeeze it over the panel's top edge.
  - Wipe in straight lines, front to back.
  - Turn an upper-panel towel to a fresh face before it shares.
  - Give every lower panel its own towel.
  - Do the rockers and mirrors last, then retire that towel.
  - Set every used towel aside. Never back in the bucket.
  - Rinse any panel that starts drying.
  - Stop the timer when the last panel is done.
