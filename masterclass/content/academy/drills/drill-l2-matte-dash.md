needs:
  - Meguiar's Quik Interior Detailer
  - Interior towels
  - A dry microfiber for the screens
  - A timer
instructions:
  - Start the timer.
  - Spray the detailer on the towel, never the panel.
  - Wipe the hard surfaces top down.
  - Wipe the screens with a dry microfiber.
  - Blot any mark on the headliner with a barely damp towel.
  - Clean the steering wheel and the pedals, then dry them.
  - Dress the plastics matte.
  - text: Which plastic dressing to use is not named yet (pending).
    status: verify
  - Sit in the driver's seat and look at the windshield for glare off the dash.
  - Touch the steering wheel and the pedals for slick.
  - Stop the timer.
