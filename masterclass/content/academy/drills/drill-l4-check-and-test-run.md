instructions:
  - Unplug the polisher.
  - Check the center bolt is tight.
  - Check the pad is centered on the backing plate.
  - Check the hooks are not bald or melted.
  - Spin the plate by hand and watch the edge for a crack or a warp.
  - Look for loose hardware, misaligned or binding parts, and cracked or broken parts.
  - Plug it in. Hold it off the car.
  - Set the dial to the highest speed.
  - Run it for at least 30 seconds. Time it.
  - Listen and feel for abnormal noise or vibration.
  - Abnormal noise or vibration? Stop. Set it aside. It gets fixed before anyone uses it.
  - text: Turn the dial down to speed 1 to 2 before the pad goes near paint.
