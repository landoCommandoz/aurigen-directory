needs:

instructions:
  - Hold the light low across the paint.
  - Walk the car, panel by panel.
  - Find every flaw you can.
  - Fingernail test each scratch.
  - Photograph each flaw before you touch the panel.
  - "Write each flaw down: the panel, what it is, and whether your nail catches."
  - Compare your map with Lando's.
