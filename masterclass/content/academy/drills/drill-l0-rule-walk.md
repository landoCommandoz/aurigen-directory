## Breaks Lando can stage

Each one breaks a rule from the 13. Pick 5.

- A bottle of tire shine on the cart for a polish job (rule 5).
- A sharp body line inside a polish square with no tape (rule 9).
- A clay bar on the floor, then put back in its case (rule 8).
- A pad with paint color on it, set back on the machine (rule 10).
- A job sheet with no before photos, and the hose already out (rule 13).
- A job sheet with no test spot logged, and the polisher plugged in (rule 4).
- The polisher set up on a panel in full sun (rule 6).
- A tire shine bottle next to a motorcycle (rule 11).

## Why

On a busy Saturday you will not remember the reason for every rule. You only need to see the break and stop it. This drill trains your eyes.
