needs:
  - A sandwich bag
  - The clay bar, torn in half, one piece in the case
  - "The clay tub: 1 gal water + 1 oz Citrus Wash & Gloss"
  - A washed car
  - Lando, with his own bag
instructions:
  - Put your hand in the sandwich bag.
  - Glide it over four panels Lando picks.
  - "Call each panel out loud. Gritty: clay it. Smooth: leave it."
  - Lando checks each panel with his own bag.
  - Pick one gritty panel.
  - Flatten your clay piece into a palm-size patty.
  - Work one 2 x 2 ft section at a time.
  - Soak the section with lube from the clay tub.
  - Glide in straight lines with light pressure.
  - Keep the section wet. Never let it go dry.
  - Listen and feel until the clay glides silent and smooth.
  - Knead the clay to a clean face after the panel.
  - Bag test the panel again.
  - Drop the clay? It goes in the trash. Use the other half.
