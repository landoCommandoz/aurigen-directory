Chrome gets chrome polish, then a coat of ceramic wax. Meguiar's has not confirmed the spray for chrome, so that step shows as unverified. The paint gets ceramic wax too, unless it is matte.

Nothing slick goes on the brake rotors, the grips, the pegs, or the seat, and no tire shine ever goes on a motorcycle. Not the sidewall, not near it.
