Two rules carry the bike wash. The bike must be stone cold, because water on hot pipes stains chrome and bug remover on a hot exhaust bakes on. And the hose stays on low, with no pressure at the chain, the wheel bearings, the electrics, under the seat, or the air intake.

The soap, the mix, and the wash method for a bike are not in the knowledge base yet, so that step shows as unverified until Lando sets it.
