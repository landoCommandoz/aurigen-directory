Pads get washed the same night, before the polish dries in. Dried compound in a pad is a dead pad. Warm water, not hot, because hot water is hard on foam. Press the water out and never wring. Dry face down on a rack, fully dry before the next use.

Towels go in three piles that never mix: water-only, product, and wheels. Product towels soak an hour in warm soapy water first. Hot water for the product piles, warm for the water-only pile. Never fabric softener, dryer sheets, or bleach, and never with cotton. Dry on low or air dry: dryer heat is what melts microfiber, not the washer.

Wash water stays out of storm drains. Dump the buckets in a sink or toilet.

The HF finishing pads have their own line in the 56367 manual: machine wash cold, machine dry on medium. That is Harbor Freight's guidance for its own pads, so Lando decides which applies to them. Uro-Tec care has not been checked with Buff and Shine.
