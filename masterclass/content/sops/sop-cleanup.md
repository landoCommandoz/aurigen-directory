Pads get washed the same night, before the polish dries in. Dried compound in a pad is a dead pad. Warm water, never hot: hot water is hard on foam, and Buff and Shine says the same. Press the water out and never wring.

How pads dry is not settled. The house dries them face down on a rack. Buff and Shine says air-dry them flat, never face down on any surface, and never in the dryer. Lando decides. Fully dry before the next use either way.

Towels go in three piles that never mix: water-only, product, and wheels. Product towels soak an hour in warm soapy water first. The house washes the product piles hot and the water-only pile warm. The Rag Company, a towel maker, says warm or cold, never hot. Follow the care tag on your towels until Lando decides. Never fabric softener, dryer sheets, or bleach, and never with cotton. Dry on low or air dry.

Wash water stays out of storm drains. Dump the buckets in a sink or toilet. That is a house rule.

The HF finishing pads have their own line in the 56367 manual: machine wash cold, machine dry on medium. That is Harbor Freight's guidance for its own pads, so Lando decides which applies to them.
