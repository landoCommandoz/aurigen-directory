Protect the same day you polish. Fresh polished paint is bare.

This is a spray, not a paste or liquid wax. On a wet car: spray, rinse, dry. No buffing and no waiting, and Meguiar's says it needs no cure time. The other way is wax as you dry: a light mist on a wet panel, then dry it with a clean towel and skip the second rinse.

Streaks or high spots: put 2 to 3 sprays on a clean damp towel and re-wipe, in the shade on a cool panel.

Meguiar's recommends two coats. The house plan is a whole-car coat the next day, which counts as the second coat on a freshly polished panel.

The time plan for a full detail gives ceramic wax 30 minutes.
