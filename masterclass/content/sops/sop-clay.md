Washing leaves bonded contamination behind. The bag test decides which panels get clayed: gritty means clay it, smooth panels skip it.

A dropped clay bar goes in the trash. That rule never bends. Tearing the bar in half and keeping one piece in the case means a drop costs one piece, not the whole bar.

Soak the section with lube, never let it go dry, and keep the pressure light. Listen and feel: grabbing and noise means contamination; silent and smooth means clean. If the clay grabs or leaves marks, use more lube and lighter pressure, and knead it to a clean face.

Clay can mar soft or black paint, so plan a polish after clay.

The clay mitt has its own care. Ragnify says use it only after the car is washed, rinse a dropped mitt with water until the grit is gone, and after the job rinse it with soap and water and let it air dry. Check it before it touches paint again. On a Tesla, Tesla's manual says avoid washing mitts, so the clay mitt on a Tesla waits for Lando's decision.
