The Tesla tape list is longer than most cars. Satin black window trim turns chalky white if polish gets on it. The five cameras, the flush handle edges, the badges, the black glass header, and the textured cladding all get tape before the pad touches paint.

This list is for Lando's 2022 Model Y. Other years and models have different cameras and trim, so check each car.
