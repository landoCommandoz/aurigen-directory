The Tesla tape list is longer than most cars. Keep polish off the satin black window trim: Tesla's manual says polish can damage chrome and metal finishes, and Meguiar's says Ultimate Compound is not for satin finishes. The cameras, the flush handle edges, the badges, the black glass header, and the textured cladding all get tape before the pad touches paint.

A 2020 to 2024 Model Y, like Lando's 2022, has five body cameras to tape. A 2025 or newer Model Y has six: the sixth sits on the front bumper above the grille. An untaped camera takes polish, and Tesla says no abrasives on a lens. Other models have different cameras and trim, so check each car.
