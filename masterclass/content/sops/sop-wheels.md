Wheels and tires go first because the cleaner splashes. Do them after the paint and the cleaner lands on panels you just cleaned.

The label is the rule here. It says wear eye protection and rubber gloves, wet and cool the wheel first, and let the foam work 30 seconds. It says never use it on a warm or damaged wheel, a wheel with damaged clear coat, or the brakes, and to test a hidden spot first. If the wheel hazes or spots, stop.

The cleaner has to come off before it dries. That is why the wheel must be cool and why you finish one wheel before you spray the next. Spray all four at once and the last one has dried before you get to it.

Whatever comes off a wheel stays with the wheels: wheel brushes never touch paint or the wash bucket, and wheel towels stay wheel towels.
