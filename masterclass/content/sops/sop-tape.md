Tape keeps the pad and the polish off the trim, the emblems, and the sharp body lines. White residue on trim or badges comes from untaped trim.

Edges and body lines always read low on the gauge, so you stay an inch off them with the pad, and the tape marks the line.

The time plan for a full detail gives tape 10 minutes. The one-step day plan gives it 20.
