A motorcycle windshield is plastic, not glass. National Cycle and Harley-Davidson both say no alcohol, no glass cleaner, and nothing abrasive, so no compound. No clay is a house rule. Do not clean it in hot sun.

The approved plastic cleaner is not chosen yet. National Cycle says warm water and a soft cloth, with a non-abrasive soap such as dishwashing liquid if needed. Dish soap sits on the house never-on-paint list, so Lando decides.
