Utah water is hard. Let it dry on the paint and it leaves spots. So the rinse and the dry happen back to back, and you never walk away from a wet car in the sun.

The final rinse is the open hose with the nozzle off, letting the water sheet off top down. Then the compressor goes first, into every gap, and the towels come after: laid flat and pulled toward you. Then open every door and dry the jambs.

The time plan for a full detail gives rinse, dry, and jambs 20 minutes.
