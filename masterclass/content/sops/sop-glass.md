Glass is last: the last thing inside and the last thing outside.

Two towels, one wet and one dry, and never a towel that touched wax or polish. Outside, wipe up and down. Inside, wipe side to side. Then a streak tells you which side it is on.

The inside glass is the last step of the interior stage. This stage is the outside.
