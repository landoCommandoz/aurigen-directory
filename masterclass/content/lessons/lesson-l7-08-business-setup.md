**Goal:** Know each line of the business setup checklist, what the agencies say, and which lines wait on Lando.

Every line here is a legal or money fact. The knowledge base marks the whole checklist unverified. The fact-checker read the agencies' own pages for most lines, and those lines show as sourced. The rest wait on a phone call, an agent's answer, or Lando's decision.

The agencies' pages are reference tools, not the law itself, and the Utah Code text was not read. Ask the agency when a line matters to a decision.
