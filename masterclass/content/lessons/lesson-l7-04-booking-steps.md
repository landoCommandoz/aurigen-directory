**Goal:** Run every booking through the same seven steps, from the first call to the keys going back.

The same seven steps every time mean nothing is promised that the paint cannot deliver. The test spot comes before the quote, and the script comes before anything touches the car.

The walkaround and the script are taught in full in Level 3. The aftercare card is part of pickup, but its wording waits on Lando.
