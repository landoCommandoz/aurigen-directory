**Goal:** Check the car with the light the way the customer will, before the keys go back.

The customer judges the job in a few minutes at pickup. The final walkaround is your chance to see it first, with the same light and the same eyes.

The aftercare card is part of the handoff, but its wording is customer content and waits on Lando. Nothing about it is written here.
