**Goal:** Protect polished paint the same day, with the spray on a wet car, and plan the second coat.

Polishing takes off a thin layer of clear coat and leaves the paint bare. That is why protection goes on the same day, every time.

This is a spray, used on a wet car. The full lesson will walk through the standard way, wax as you dry, and the second coat. The drying-aid idea stays unconfirmed until a Meguiar's source backs it.
