**Goal:** Keep slick products off until the paint is done, tape trim before polishing, and know that faded trim needs a restorer the kit does not have yet.

Trim gets two jobs. Before polishing, it gets tape, so polish never dries on it. After the paint is done, faded trim can be restored.

The kit has no trim restorer yet, and the residue removal method is not confirmed. Both show with the unverified badge until a product is chosen and its label is read.
