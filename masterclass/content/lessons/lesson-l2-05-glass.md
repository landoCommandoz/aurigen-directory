**Goal:** Clean glass last with two clean towels and a direction rule, so any streak tells you which side it is on.

## Why glass is last

Every other step can put something on the glass: overspray from the dash, or dust you knock down from the headliner. Do the glass first and you do it twice. The rule is the same inside and out. Glass is the last thing inside and the last thing outside.

## Why two towels

One wet towel cleans. One dry towel finishes. Fix It lists two causes of glass streaks, most likely first: a towel that touched wax or polish, and a one-towel wipe. The two-towel rule and clean towels take both off the table.

## Why the direction rule works

Inside, you wipe side to side. Outside, you wipe up and down. When you step back and see a streak, its direction tells you which side of the glass it is on. A side-to-side streak is inside. An up-and-down streak is outside. Without the rule you guess, and you wipe the wrong side.

## The edges people miss

Drop each window 1 to 2 inches and clean the top edge. Do the windshield from the passenger seat to reach the far corner. Those are the two places that get missed.

## Teslas and bikes

On a Tesla, the camera lenses are not settled. The house line is glass cleaner only. Tesla's manual says spray water on the lens and never use chemical-based or abrasive cleaners on it. On a 2020 to 2024 Model Y built before about late May 2023 in Fremont or Austin, it says never wipe the lens with a hand or a cloth, because grit can scratch it. Lando's 2022 is in that group unless its build date says otherwise. Lando decides how this applies to Teslas.

On a bike, the windshield is plastic. No alcohol and no glass cleaner; the windshield maker says so. The approved plastic cleaner is not confirmed yet, so that row carries an unverified badge.
