**Goal:** Clean the outside glass last, with two clean towels and a direction rule, so a streak tells you its side.

Glass comes last so nothing else lands on it after you clean it. Wax and polish on a towel smear glass, so glass gets its own clean towels.

The direction rule is the trick. Outside goes up and down, inside goes side to side. When you see a streak, its direction tells you which side to wipe again.
