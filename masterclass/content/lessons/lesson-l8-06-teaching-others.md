**Goal:** Teach a new hire with the drills, watch the owner checks, and keep every sign-off behind your PIN.

A sign-off is a record that you watched the skill done right. That is why it lives on the shop phone, behind your PIN, and never on a study phone.

Level 8 opens when 25 cars are logged. By then you have done every skill yourself many times, and you can teach it.
