**Goal:** Know every price on the car and bike menus, the fees on top, and which lines are not settled yet.

The menu is the same every time, so every customer hears the same price for the same work. List prices are drop-off prices. Mobile is a convenience fee on top, never a discount for dropping off.

Two lines wait on Lando: what the monthly plan includes, and whether prices include sales tax. The chain add-on also waits on him for bikes with a belt.
