**Goal:** Wash a car roof to rockers with the 16-towel method so a dirty towel never goes back in the bucket.

## The whole idea in one line

A dirty towel never goes back in the bucket. Everything else in this method serves that one rule.

## Why towels, not a mitt

The old way was one mitt and two buckets, and the same mitt goes back in the water after every panel. With the towel method, each towel works one panel and comes out of the rotation. A dirty towel never goes back in, so the bucket stays clean all the way to the last panel.

You start with 16 microfiber towels in the bucket, one per panel, folded in quarters. Lift a towel out dripping and squeeze it over the top edge of the panel. Wipe in straight lines, front to back. Never circles. When the towel is done, it goes in the used pile, never back in the bucket.

## Upper panels share, lower panels never do

Upper panels are the cleanest. They may share a towel if you turn it to a fresh face. Lower panels carry the heaviest dirt, so each gets its own towel. Rockers and mirrors come last, and then that towel is retired.

Work roof to rockers, one panel at a time. Clean panels come first, so you never carry low dirt up onto a high panel.

## Keep it wet

Utah water spots when it dries on paint. Keep the whole car wet the entire time. If a panel starts drying, rinse it. The hard water lesson picks this up.

## The bucket depends on the job

Before a polish, the bucket is 4 gal water and 4 oz CG Citrus Wash & Gloss. That is 1 oz per gallon, the house strip strength, because polishing follows. Chemical Guys says 1 oz per 5-gal bucket for a regular wash. Its strip figure reads as about 3 oz per 5-gal bucket, but that number is not confirmed until Lando reads the strip line on his 64 oz bottle. The house ratio stays the same either way.

On a maintenance wash, Meguiar's soaps run about 1 oz per gallon, per a Meguiar's specialist. Super Soap goes to 2 oz per gallon when you want it stronger.

Ultimate Wash & Wax is for maintenance washes only, never before a polish. Dish soap, Spic and Span, wheel brushes, and the clay mitt never go in the wash bucket.

## The two-bucket alternative

The two-bucket mitt wash is still allowed as the alternative: a mitt and two grit guards, rinse the mitt after every panel, and a dropped mitt is done for the day. Break a new mitt in with soapy water on the windshield for a minute or two.

## Teslas

On Lando's Model Y the wash route is: roof, windshield, rear glass, hood, liftgate, L fender, L front door, L rear door, L quarter, R quarter, R rear door, R front door, R fender, front bumper, rear bumper.
