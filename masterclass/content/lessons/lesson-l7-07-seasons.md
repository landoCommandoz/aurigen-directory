**Goal:** Sell what the season allows: interiors and washes in winter, and Store-It-Clean for bikes in October.

Utah weather sets the calendar. Corrections need a cool panel that is not freezing, so winter is for interiors and washes.

Bikes go into storage in the fall. The October pitch is to store them clean, because dirt left on all winter pits chrome and etches paint.
