**Goal:** Empty the car the same way every time, keep every personal item where the customer can see it, and follow the top-down order.

## Why the order never changes

The interior runs in one order on every car: everything out, then the mats, then knock it down from the top, then vacuum top down, then wipe the hard surfaces top down, then the spots, then dress the plastics matte, then the glass last.

The reason is simple. Whatever you knock loose falls. If you vacuum first and then knock dust out of the vents, it lands on the carpet you just cleaned, and you vacuum twice. Top down means every step only ever drops dirt onto a surface you have not cleaned yet. Glass goes last because every other step can put a smear or a drip on it.

## Why the customer's things go in one bag

People leave real things in their cars: cash, cards, sunglasses, a kid's toy that matters more than the car. The rule is one bag the customer can see. Money or valuables go on the dash, and you say something.

This protects you as much as them. If nothing is hidden and nothing is moved somewhere they cannot find it, nobody can say you lost or took anything. A bag they can see at pickup ends that conversation before it starts.

## Why the mats come out first

Mats hold most of the dirt in a car. They come out with everything else, get hosed, brushed, rinsed, and stood in the sun. In a full detail they soak while you do the wheels, so they have the whole wash to dry. If they come out last, they go back in wet.

## Two things the knowledge base has not settled

Putting a child's car seat back in is not covered either. That one matters, so it is flagged for Lando and not guessed at here.

## The time

A clean daily driver takes about 1 hr 5 min inside. A family car with pet hair, crumbs, and stains takes 2 hrs or more. That gap is why interiors are priced by condition, and why whoever quotes looks in the back seat first.
