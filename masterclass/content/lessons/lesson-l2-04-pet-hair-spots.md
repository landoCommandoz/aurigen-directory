**Goal:** Pull pet hair with the rubber tool before you vacuum, handle spots without guessing at a product, and get air on wet carpet before handoff.

## Why the rubber tool goes first

The knowledge base gives one order for pet hair: the rubber tool, the Analan, or pumice first, then the vacuum, three passes minimum. The tool loosens the hair, and the vacuum takes what the tool loosened. Vacuum first and you are working against the hair instead of with the tool.

## Why pet hair is its own price

A family car with pet hair, crumbs, and stains takes 2 hrs or more inside. A clean daily driver takes about 1 hr 5 min. The extra time is real, so pet hair is quoted separately as a $50 add-on, and heavy mess is $75+.

## Why spots wait on Lando

The knowledge base puts spots in the interior order, after the wipe and before the dressing. It does not say how to treat a spot or with which product. A guessed product is a guess made on someone else's seat.

So this lesson does not guess. The spot step carries an unverified badge until Lando sets the method and the product.

## Why wet carpet needs air

Carpet that goes back to the customer wet stays wet in a closed car. Before handoff, crack the windows or run the fan.
