**Goal:** Work out the lowest price a job can carry, and see why a discount comes out of your own pay.

The floor price adds up what the job really costs you: a share of the monthly costs, the products on this car, and your hours at your hourly rate. Any price under it is paid for out of your own pocket.

This is owner math. It stays internal, and it never shows to a customer.
