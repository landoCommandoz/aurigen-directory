**Goal:** Clean hard surfaces, screens, and the headliner with no overspray, no shine, and no soaking, and leave the wheel and pedals with zero slick.

## Why you spray the towel

Spray a panel straight on and the mist does not stay on the panel. It lands on the glass and the screens next to it, and now you have streaks to chase on surfaces you have not cleaned yet. Spray Meguiar's Quik Interior Detailer on the towel and the product only goes where the towel goes.

## Why nothing shiny on the dash

A shiny dash glares in the windshield. The driver sees the dash reflected in the glass, and that is the opposite of what a detail is for. If the dash looks wet, you used too much. The fix is a clean damp towel, then a dry one. Dress the plastics matte, never glossy.

## Why the screens get almost nothing

Screens get a dry microfiber or a screen-safe cleaner only. The knowledge base does not name a screen-safe product in the kit yet, so that row carries an unverified badge. Quik Interior Detailer is not confirmed screen-safe by Meguiar's, so it does not count.

## Teslas: the manual is stricter

Tesla's manual is stricter than the house steps, and Lando decides how it applies to Teslas. Until he does, both lines show.

- Touchscreen: turn on Screen Clean Mode, then a soft lint-free cloth made for displays. No cleaners of any kind, and no dry statically-charged cloth such as a freshly washed microfiber, which is the house tool.
- Interior parts: no alcohol, citrus, bleach, naphtha, hand sanitizer, or silicone-based products. Nobody has checked what Quik Interior Detailer contains.
- Seats: warm water and mild soap on a microfiber, then a dry wipe. No conditioners or protectants, and never spray the seat directly.

## Why the headliner gets blotted

The headliner is held up by glue. Soak it and the glue lets go. Barely damp, blot only, never scrub.

## Why zero slick on the wheel and pedals

A slick steering wheel or a slick pedal is a safety problem the moment the customer drives away. Clean them, then dry them until nothing is slick. This is the one place where how it looks matters less than how it feels.

## What is not settled

Three products are not named yet: the plastic dressing, the screen-safe cleaner, and the leather cleaner and conditioner. Each one carries an unverified badge until Lando names it. Armor All Original protectant is on the never list. It stays in the garage, so do not reach for it as a dressing.
