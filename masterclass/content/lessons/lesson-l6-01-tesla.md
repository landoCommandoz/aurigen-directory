**Goal:** Know what changes on a Tesla Model Y, and which house steps wait on Lando's Tesla decision.

Lando's own car is a 2022 Model Y, so Teslas come up often. Some changes are settled: Car Wash Mode before water, the glass roof skipped, and a longer tape list.

Other steps are not settled. Tesla's owner's manual disagrees with the house on the wheel cleaner, the pre-soak, compound, camera lenses, mitts, the hose, the touchscreen, and the interior. It also says cleaning-product damage is not covered by Tesla's warranty. Until Lando decides, every one of those shows the house line, Tesla's line, and the pending row. Never treat them as settled on a Tesla.
