**Goal:** Split a full detail between two people so no dwell time is wasted.

A full detail has waiting built in: mats soaking and product dwelling. One person spends that time waiting. Two people, one inside and one outside, fill it.

Two lines here wait on the pre-soak and the iron remover, and who may run power tools waits on Lando.
