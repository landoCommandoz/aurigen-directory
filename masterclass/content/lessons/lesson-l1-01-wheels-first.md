**Goal:** Clean all four wheels and tires first, one at a time, on a cool wheel, without letting cleaner dry or a wheel brush touch the paint.

## Why wheels go first

Wheel cleaner splashes. If you wash the paint first and the wheels second, you splash cleaner and wheel grime onto panels you just cleaned. So wheels and tires come first, before anything touches the paint.

## Why one wheel at a time, on a cool wheel

The cleaner has to come off before it dries. On a hot wheel it dries fast. If you spray all four, the last one has dried before you get to it. So the wheel must be cool, and you do one wheel at a time: spray, wait about a minute, brush, rinse before it dries, then the next.

The hand test tells you. Put your hand on the wheel. If it is hot, wait.

## Three brushes, three jobs

Soft brush on the face. Long brush in the barrel. Stiff brush on the tire. No bucket: the cleaner, a brush, and the hose.

## Wheel tools stay wheel tools

Wheel brushes never touch paint, and they never go in the wash bucket. Towels that touch a wheel go in the wheel pile for good and never rejoin the paint pile. Whatever comes off a wheel stays with the wheels.

## Teslas: not settled

On a Tesla, turn on Car Wash Mode before any water, and leave the wheel aero covers on.

The wheel cleaner is not settled on a Tesla. The house step uses Armor All Extreme Wheel & Tire on every car. Tesla's manual says do not use chemical-based wheel cleaners or pre-wash products, because they can damage the finish on the wheels, and it says damage from cleaning products is not covered by Tesla's warranty. ## The time

On a full detail, wheels take 25 min, and the floor mats soak while you do them.
