**Goal:** Know what the black pad does now, and the two jobs it may do later.

The black pad is the softest pad in the kit. It finishes and applies wax, and it never cuts. That is why it is never a rung on the ladder.

Later, it may apply a sealant by machine or give soft black paint a final gloss pass. Both are future uses, after the one-step is second nature.
