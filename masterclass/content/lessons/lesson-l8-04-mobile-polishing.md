**Goal:** Plan mobile polishing for after 25 cars, with water, power, parking, and weather settled before you go.

At home, the shop has shade, power, water, and the compressor. A customer's driveway may have none of them. Every one of them gets settled on the phone, before the day of the job.

Power near water is the safety line. The polisher's manual sets the cord and outlet rules. The generator size and the cold limit are not confirmed by any source yet.
