**Goal:** Know what a two-step is, when it opens, and what is missing before it can be taught.

Level 8 opens when 25 cars are logged. On a mid-size sedan, a two-step takes 4 h 55 m on the paint, against 2 h 30 m for a one-step. It waits until the one-step is second nature.

The knowledge base has the time column and the pad count, but no procedure and no price. This outline holds the place until Lando writes the method and the fact-checker checks it.
