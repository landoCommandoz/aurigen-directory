**Goal:** Turn the 8 checks into an honest talk, so the customer hears what polishing can and can't do before you quote.

The 8 checks protect the car and the customer. Said out loud, they also protect you: a customer who heard it before the work starts does not feel misled at pickup.

The same honesty covers scratches and price. Never promise a scratch comes out, and never cut the price for the same work.
