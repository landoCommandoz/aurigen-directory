**Goal:** Feel the house pressure on a bathroom scale before you polish, float the last set, and know the manual's line on pressure is waiting on Lando.

## Why a scale

About 10 lb is the house number. It is hard to judge by feel alone. A bathroom scale turns the number into a feeling your arms remember. Do it with the polisher unplugged, pad flat on the scale, both hands on the machine. Press until the scale reads about 10 lb. Hold it. Then ease off until the scale shows only the machine's own weight. That second feeling is the float you use on the last set.

## What too much pressure does

The pad on this machine turns on its own. Push too hard and it slows down or stops. You see it on the marker line: when the line slows or stops, ease up and flatten the pad. Lesson 4 covers the marker line.

Pressure over about 10 lb, and a grip that is too tight, are also causes of the polisher jerking, walking, or hopping side to side. The polisher jerking was one of the Oct 7 problems. The fix list includes two hands, a loose grip, and guiding the machine instead of pushing it.

## Where you use less

Three places get less than the house number.

The last set of every square floats. Let the machine ride on its own weight.

Plastic bumpers heat fast, so they get lighter pressure, along with a lower speed and a shorter time.

A panel that reads 85 to 89 µm gets light pressure and the fewest passes, after you tell the customer. The band table in Level 3 sets that, and it is a house standard.

## The manual's line, and why it is pending

The 56367 owner's manual says something different from the house number. It says keep pressure off of the tool when operating, and the foam pad should lightly contact the polishing surface.

The house number and the manual disagree. Lando decides which one governs. Until he does, the house setting stays, and every place that shows pressure shows all three lines: about 10 lb as the house line, the manual's light touch as a sourced line, and the pending decision. Do not treat either one as settled.

## Color on the pad

If you see paint color on the pad, stop. You are through the clear coat. Old single-stage paint is the only exception. On old single-stage paint, some color on the pad is normal: say it, note it, and back off pressure. Level 3 covers how to spot it before you start.
