**Goal:** Dress dry tires with a thin coat, keep it off the paint, let it dry before the car moves, and never dress a bike tire.

Tire dressing is slick. That is why it waits until the paint is polished, and why a thick coat ends up as black spots down the side of the car.

The house way and the label disagree on where you spray. The house sprays the applicator to keep overspray off the paint. The label sprays the tire and says do not wipe. Both lines show until Lando decides.
