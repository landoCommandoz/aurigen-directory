**Goal:** Refresh cloudy lenses by hand, and sell it as a refresh, never as a fix.

A headlight refresh makes the lenses brighter for now. It does not last, so the honest word is refresh.

The house times are not confirmed by the bottle. Read your bottle's directions, and never promise a customer how long the result lasts.
