**Goal:** Know the bike rules that differ from a car, starting with no tire shine on a motorcycle, ever.

A bike puts a rider on two small patches of rubber. Anything slick on a tire, a grip, a peg, or the seat can put the rider down. That is why the bike rules are stricter than the car rules.

Bikes are drop-off only, washed stone cold with the hose on low, dried with the compressor, and polished by hand. A few lines still wait on Lando: the windshield cleaner, what goes on bike wheels, the chain add-on on belt-drive bikes, and hand polishing without the Level 4 sign-off.
