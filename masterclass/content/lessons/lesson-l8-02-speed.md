**Goal:** Compare your logged times with the time map, and price from what you actually do.

The time map is the booklet's plan for a mid-size sedan in the shade. Your real times will differ. The gap between the two shows where you lose time, and your real times are what the price has to cover.

Level 8 opens when 25 cars are logged, so you have real times to compare by then.
