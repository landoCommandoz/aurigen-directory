**Goal:** Find ceramic wax streaks and high spots, and wipe them out without making more.

A streak or high spot is wax that did not spread or dry evenly. The house fix is a clean damp towel with a little more spray, in the shade, on a cool panel.

No Meguiar's page covers this fix yet, so it carries the unverified badge until a label or the maker confirms it.
