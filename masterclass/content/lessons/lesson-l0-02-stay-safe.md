**Goal:** Work so that chemicals, cords, heat, and heavy loads never hurt you, and stop the moment a tool or a panel tells you to.

## What can hurt you here

Four things in this shop can hurt you or the car: chemicals, a spinning tool and its cord, heat, and weight. This lesson gives you only the safety lines Lando's knowledge base and the polisher's manual actually say. Where nothing is confirmed yet, the line says so and carries an unverified badge. Nothing is made up to fill a gap.

## Chemicals

Read the label before you use any chemical. The label on the bottle beats this app, so it is also your first guide to gloves and first aid. The gear and first-aid lines for each product are not checked yet. The fact-checker is pulling them from each label or safety sheet.

The IPA panel wipe lives in one bottle: the Zep pump sprayer, labeled. An unlabeled bottle of clear liquid gets mistaken for something else.

## The polisher and its cord

The polisher is a BAUER 7.5 A dual action machine. It is not a rotary, but the pad still spins fast.

The cord goes over your shoulder. That keeps it off the paint and away from the pad. When you put the machine down, it rests on its side on a towel, never on its pad.

The pad goes flat on the paint before you pull the trigger. You let go of the trigger while the pad is still on the paint, then you lift. A pad that starts or stops in the air slings product. Never park it spinning in one spot.

Before you check the bolt, the pad, the hooks, or the backing plate, unplug it. The manual adds a test run: pad mounted, plugged in, highest speed, at least 30 seconds off the car. If you hear abnormal noise or feel abnormal vibration, stop using it right away and get it fixed before you use it again. That line is from the manual, not a house guess. The tool is telling you something is loose, worn, or broken.

## Heat

Heat shows up three ways. A hot wheel dries wheel cleaner before you can rinse it. A hot panel dries the polish before you can finish the section. Plastic bumpers heat fastest, so they get lighter pressure, lower speed, and shorter time.

The check is your hand. If you cannot keep your hand on the panel, stop and let it cool. On a bike, the whole bike must be stone cold. Water on hot pipes stains chrome, and bug remover on a hot exhaust bakes on.

## When the car tells you to stop

Paint color on the pad means stop. You are through the clear coat. Old single-stage paint is the only exception. Stopping there protects the customer's car, and it protects you from paying for a repaint.

## Riders

After a bike wash, the brakes are wet. Tell every rider the first stop is soft, and to ride easy for a block.

## What is not set yet

Eye protection, outdoor outlets and extension cords, the heat gun, compressed air, and lifting have no confirmed rule yet. The power numbers for mobile jobs (household outlet, 12-gauge cord, the generator) are not confirmed either. Each of these shows as pending until the fact-checker sources it from the manual, the label, or a safety agency, or Lando sets a house rule.
