**Goal:** Run a full interior in order, check it the way the customer will, and log your time against about 1 hr 5 min for a clean daily driver.

## Why this lesson is the sign-off

The first five lessons of this level each teach one piece. This one puts them together on a real car, in order, with the clock running. Lando signs off the interior skill when he watches you do the whole thing. Exactly what he watches for lives in the sign-off file, not here.

## The order, once more

Everything out. Mats. Knock it down from the top. Vacuum top down, with the seats slid forward and back. Wipe the hard surfaces top down, spraying the towel and never the panel. Spots. Dress the plastics matte. Glass last. Air on wet carpet before handoff.

Every step drops dirt only onto something you have not cleaned yet. Skip a step or swap two, and you clean something twice.

## Check it the way the customer will

The customer does not see your work from the door. They sit in the driver's seat, put their hands on the wheel, and look through the windshield. So check the dash for shine, because shine glares in the windshield. Check the wheel and the pedals for slick. Check the glass for streaks. Make sure the bag with their things is where they can see it. Then look in the back seat, the same place you looked before the quote.

## Why the clock matters

A clean daily driver takes about 1 hr 5 min inside. A family car with pet hair, crumbs, and stains takes 2 hrs or more. Interior Only is $150 on a sedan and $180 on an SUV or truck at drop-off. If the job runs twice as long as the plan, the price did not cover it. That is why interiors are priced by condition, and why you log your real time on every one.

## What happened on October 7

On Lando's second session, setup took most of the time, the interior got cut, and he still ran out of time. The fix in the field notes: split personal-car work across days, and let the Job Runner show a cutoff time. For customer cars, Fix It says to quote the real hours.

## In a full detail

The interior runs in the gaps of the outside work. The vacuum takes 25 min, the interior wipe 20 min, and the interior glass 10 min. One person straight through takes 7 h 10 m for a full detail. Working the dwell takes 6 h 30 m. Two people, one in and one out, take about 5 h.
