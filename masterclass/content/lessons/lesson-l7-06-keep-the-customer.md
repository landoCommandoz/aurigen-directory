**Goal:** Leave every pickup with one offer, a review ask, and one referral ask.

The best time to ask is while the customer is looking at a clean car. One offer is easy to say yes to. Three offers sound like a sales pitch.

The monthly plan is still being defined, so its contents carry the unverified badge.
