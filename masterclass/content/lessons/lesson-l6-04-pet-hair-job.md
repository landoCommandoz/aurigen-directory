**Goal:** Pull pet hair with the rubber tool before the vacuum, and quote it as its own add-on.

Pet hair weaves into carpet and fabric. A vacuum alone skims the top. The rubber tool pulls it loose first, then the vacuum takes it away.

A car full of pet hair takes far longer than a clean daily driver. That is why you look in the back seat before you quote, and why pet hair is its own line on the price.
