**Goal:** Know when the shade, heat, wind, or cold says not today, and what you do instead.

Polish and protection both need a cool panel out of the sun and wind. Your hand is the test: if you cannot keep it on the panel, the panel is too hot.

The house cold limit is not confirmed by any label yet, so it carries the unverified badge. When it is too cold, the house answer is to garage the car or move the date.
