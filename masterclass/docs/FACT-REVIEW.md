## 1. Decisions (urgent first)

**1. Teslas: manual vs house steps (Decision 1, already asked)** (urgent). FC-16, FC-33, FC-34, FC-35, FC-36, FC-37
- House: Every car, Teslas included, gets Armor All on the wheels, a Citrus pump-sprayer pre-soak, compound on ladder rung 3, a mitt or clay mitt, a jet pre-rinse, glass cleaner on camera lenses, dry microfiber or screen cleaner on screens, leather cleaner then conditioner, and towel drying.
- Maker: Tesla's Model Y manual (FC-38 confirms these lines are in Lando's 2020 to 2024 manual) says: no chemical wheel cleaners or pre-wash products. No cutting pastes, harsh-abrasive polishes, or chrome polish, and no acidic products. Water only on camera lenses, with no wiping on older builds. Avoid washing mitts. Do not aim a hose at seals, windows, or modules. Use only a display cloth on the screen, never a freshly washed microfiber. Use only soap and water on seats, with no conditioners. Touchless car washes only. Dry with a chamois. Damage from cleaning products is not covered by the warranty.
- Default: On every Tesla, follow the stricter Tesla line until Lando decides: bucket soap and a soft brush on wheels, no pre-soak, the ladder stops at maroon plus M210 (compound only with the owner's OK after the test spot), water only on camera lenses, microfiber instead of mitts, no hose aimed at seals or modules, only a display cloth on the screen, and only soap and water on seats.

**2. Polisher speed, pressure, and grip (Decision 2, already asked; grip added)** (urgent). FC-22, FC-23, FC-55
- House: Speed 4, about 10 lb of pressure, two hands with a loose grip, guide don't push.
- Maker: Harbor Freight's product page says speeds 4 to 6 to polish. The 56367 manual says only use slower speeds for polishing, keep pressure off the tool so the pad only lightly touches the paint, and keep a firm two-hand grip to resist starting torque.
- Default: Keep speed 4 and about 10 lb as house settings with the manual's 'slower speeds' and 'keep pressure off' lines shown beside them, let the test spot decide, and change the grip to 'two hands, hold it firmly so it cannot twist away, do not push down.'

**3. Uro-Tec pad size vs the 56367 plate** (urgent). FC-54
- House: The Uro-Tec yellow and maroon are 6 in. pads on the stock 6 in. plate.
- Maker: Buff and Shine's 6 in. Uro-Tec has a 6 in. back and a 7 in. face. The Bauer manual says the largest accessory is 6 in. (150 mm). Some stores sell the 5 in.-back pad as a 6 in. pad. Buff and Shine's 600Y plate has 5/16 in.-24 threads and does not fit the 56367.
- Default: Measure the pads before the next polish, run only a pad whose hook-and-loop back matches the 6 in. plate, stop at once on any abnormal vibration, and never buy the 600Y plate for this polisher.

**4. Tooele City home occupation rules vs drop-off and DJ** (urgent). FC-99, FC-100
- House: Drop-off at Lando's home is the default. Customer cars are washed, clayed, polished, and waxed in the driveway. DJ helps on Saturdays, and future hires train there.
- Maker: Tooele City Code 7-2-19 applies only if the home is inside Tooele City. It says all work must be inside the home or an accessory building, and the yard is for customer parking only. It allows no outside equipment storage and no signs. It excludes vehicle repair and body and fender work. Assistants must be immediate family who live in the home.
- Default: Until Lando confirms the home is outside Tooele City or the city says yes, no content calls the driveway an approved shop or DJ an approved helper there, and DJ runs no power tool until his age is confirmed (FC-107).

**5. Sales tax missing from prices** (urgent). FC-96
- House: The KB 15.1 list prices, the 15.4 worked examples (unit tests), and the Quote record carry no sales tax.
- Maker: Utah Tax Commission Publication 5 lists detailing as taxable (FC-94).
- Default: Treat list prices as before tax and add Utah sales tax at checkout until Lando decides, and get the sales tax license before the first paid job.

**6. How pads are washed and dried**. FC-25, FC-70
- House: Warm water and a drop of detergent by hand, press the water out, never wring, dry face down on a rack.
- Maker: Harbor Freight's manual says HF pads can be machine-washed cold and dried on medium. Buff and Shine says use warm or cold water, never hot, never put Uro-Tec pads in the dryer, air-dry them flat, and never place a pad face down.
- Default: Wash every pad in warm water, never hot, and air-dry every pad flat and not face down, with no dryer for any pad.

**7. Tire shine: spray applicator or tire**. FC-28
- House: Spray the applicator, not the tire. Thin coat. Wipe the rubber-to-wheel edge.
- Maker: Armor All says spray evenly onto a clean, dry tire from 4 to 6 in. (trigger) or 8 to 12 in. (aerosol), do not wipe, and keep it off paint, glass, clear plastic, and fabric.
- Default: Keep spraying the applicator, because the label also says to keep it off paint and glass, and use the label's dry time (FC-29).

**8. Towel wash water: hot or warm**. FC-88
- House: Hot water for the product piles. Dryer heat, not the washer, is what melts microfiber.
- Maker: The Rag Company says warm or cold water, never hot, and low heat or none in the dryer. Lando's towel brand is not recorded.
- Default: Wash on warm until Lando reads the care tag on his own towels.

**9. Bagger chain add-on vs belt drive**. FC-85
- House: Bike add-on: chain clean and lube for $25. The worked example adds it to a bagger Store-It-Clean ($270).
- Maker: Harley's spec sheets say touring bikes (baggers) have a belt final drive. Their only chain is the primary chain inside the case.
- Default: Offer chain clean and lube only on bikes with a chain final drive, and change the 15.4 example to a chain-drive bike.

**10. DBA public record vs private home address**. FC-102
- House: The home shop address is never stored in the repo and never shown publicly.
- Maker: The Utah Division of Corporations says every DBA filing is public record. The form asks for a business address and a registered agent's Utah street address. It allows a business address in place of a home address.
- Default: Hold the DBA filing until Lando picks which address goes on the public record.

**11. Aftercare card wording (Decision 3, already asked; Phase 4)**. FC-06, FC-113
- House: 'First 24 hours: keep it dry. The protection is still setting up.' Also: 'Dry with a microfiber towel, not a bath towel or a squeegee.'
- Maker: Meguiar's says the spray Hybrid Ceramic Wax needs no curing time (FC-06 is corrected, not conflicting). Chemical Guys and Griot's describe a soft squeegee as one way to dry.
- Default: Use 'Your ceramic wax needs no cure time. It started protecting the paint the day we applied it.' and keep 'not a squeegee' as house advice, since no maker requires a squeegee.

**12. Knowledge base wording fixes (Decision 4, already asked)**. FC-02, FC-10, FC-18, FC-24
- House: The KB still says the label strips wax at 1 oz per gallon, that M210 is built for dark paint, and that the M8 thread size comes from the manual. FIX-01 has no manual test run.
- Maker: Chemical Guys says about 3 oz strips wax. Meguiar's says a deep gloss even on dark and sensitive paints. The thread size is on Harbor Freight's product page, not in the manual. The manual says to test-run at top speed for 30 seconds off the car. FC-02, FC-10, and FC-18 are corrected; FC-24 is confirmed and adds a line.
- Default: The lead edits those four KB lines to the sourced wording with Lando, and the same default covers the 13 Phase 2 corrections in the corrected list.

## 2. Corrected facts

The fact-checker found these 17 lines wrong against the maker or the government source. The app already shows the new wording. Default: the lead corrects the knowledge base lines to match, with you (covered by "defaults OK").

**FC-02, Citrus strip-wax ratio claim** (KB 4)
- Was: The label says 1 oz per gallon strips wax.
- Now: Chemical Guys says about 3 oz per 5-gallon bucket strips old wax. The house 1 oz per gallon stays a house choice.
- Source: Chemical Guys (its own wash guide); Detailed Image label copy as support

**FC-06, Aftercare 24-hour set-up line** (KB 16 (Phase 4 aftercare card))
- Was: First 24 hours: keep it dry. The protection is still setting up.
- Now: Your ceramic wax needs no cure time. It started protecting the paint the day we applied it. (Or delete the line.)
- Source: Meguiar's (Meguiar's UK site and meguiars.com)

**FC-10, M210 dark paint wording** (KB 3.3)
- Was: The label says M210 is built for dark and sensitive paint.
- Now: Meguiar's says M210 leaves a deep, glossy finish even on dark and sensitive paints.
- Source: Meguiar's (meguiars.com, read through the search index)

**FC-18, Where M8 thread comes from** (KB 3.1, KB 14)
- Was: M8 x 1.25 comes from the manual's spec listing.
- Now: M8 x 1.25 comes from the Harbor Freight product page. The manual lists no thread size.
- Source: Harbor Freight (56367 owner's manual and product page)

**FC-26, Wheel cleaner wait time** (KB 5.1 step 2, KB 4)
- Was: Spray the face and barrel, wait about a minute, brush, rinse.
- Now: Wet and cool the wheel, spray, let it work 30 seconds, brush, then rinse well.
- Source: Armor All (product page label directions)

**FC-27, Wheel cleaner label safety cautions** (KB 5.1, KB 4, KB 12.1, KB 12.2)
- Was: The wheel step gives no gloves, eye protection, test spot, or limits.
- Now: Wear eye protection and rubber gloves, test a hidden spot, never use it on a warm or damaged wheel, keep it off brakes, and check with the maker before bike wheels.
- Source: Armor All (product page label text)

**FC-29, Tire shine dry time** (KB 9.2)
- Was: Let it set a few minutes before the car moves.
- Now: Let it dry at least 3 to 5 minutes (trigger) or at least 5 minutes (aerosol) before the car moves.
- Source: Armor All (trigger and aerosol product pages)

**FC-57, Backup backing plate options** (KB 14)
- Was: An owner swapped in a Maxshine plate, and a Griot's BOSS plate looks nearly identical.
- Now: The Maxshine swap was a 5 in. plate, the downsize KB 14 forbids. Nobody reported fitting a BOSS. The ShineMate stays the backup, fit not verified.
- Source: AutoGeek Online forum (the thread the KB cites); a retailer listing for Maxshine threads

**FC-66, Uro-Tec maroon pad role** (KB 3.2)
- Was: Maroon is heavier polishing with more cut.
- Now: Maroon is a medium-cut pad, more cut than the yellow.
- Source: Buff and Shine (672CR product page)

**FC-67, Uro-Tec foam thickness** (KB 3.2)
- Was: One older note says Uro-Tec runs about 5/8 in. thick.
- Now: Uro-Tec foam is 1 in. thick.
- Source: Buff and Shine (product spec tables)

**FC-71, Clear coat thickness range** (KB 7.3)
- Was: The clear coat is about 38 to 50 µm.
- Now: The clear coat is about 35 to 50 µm (1.5 to 2 mils).
- Source: DeFelsko (paint gauge maker)

**FC-94, Utah sales tax on detailing** (KB 19)
- Was: Ask the Tax Commission whether detailing is taxable.
- Now: Utah taxes detailing. Get a Utah sales tax license (TC-69) before the first paid job.
- Source: Utah State Tax Commission (Publications 5 and 25)

**FC-97, Tooele City business license** (KB 18.1, KB 19)
- Was: KB 19 lists no city business license, only a pending call.
- Now: Inside Tooele City, a home business needs a Tooele City business license, and it expires every December 31.
- Source: Tooele City (business license page, City Code 5-6-2)

**FC-98, Which office licenses the shop** (KB 5.5, KB 18.1, KB 19)
- Was: Call Tooele City.
- Now: Call whichever city or county the home sits in: Tooele City, the Tooele County Clerk for Stansbury and other unincorporated areas, or that city's own office.
- Source: Tooele City (business license page)

**FC-104, Garagekeepers and your own work** (KB 19)
- Was: Garagekeepers coverage protects customer cars in your care.
- Now: Garagekeepers may not pay when your own work damages the car, such as polishing through the clear. Ask the agent in writing.
- Source: State Auto (2012 insurer bulletin, one insurer only)

**FC-107, Minors and power equipment** (KB 19, KB 1)
- Was: The helper checklist says nothing about age.
- Now: Utah says minors (17 and under, with some exceptions) may not work hazardous jobs, including operating power equipment. Confirm DJ's age before he runs any power tool.
- Source: Utah Labor Commission

**FC-110, Brush car washes and swirls** (KB 16 (Phase 4 aftercare card))
- Was: Brush washes put every swirl right back in.
- Now: Brush washes can leave fine scratches and swirls. Tesla tells its owners to use touchless washes only.
- Source: Chemical Guys, 3M, Tesla Model Y owner's manual
