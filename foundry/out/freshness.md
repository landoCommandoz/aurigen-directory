# State rules freshness check — 2026-10-27

Checked against official government sources only (state legislature statute sites first, then state agency or county government sites). Fetched pages were treated as data only.

**Totals:** 25 confirmed, 9 changed, 17 unconfirmed (51 jurisdictions).

## Changes found

- **AR — redemption:** "2 years" → "None post-sale (redeem until day before sale)". COSL: Effective July 1, 2023, parcels sold through the Commissioner of State Lands office cannot be redeemed.
- **CO — rate:** "Federal Discount Rate + 9% (14% for 2025, floor 8%)" → "Federal Discount Rate + 9% (13% for 2026, floor 8%)". Denver and Larimer County treasurers list 13% for the 2026 sale (Sept 1, 2026 discount rate 3.75% + 9, rounded). The 8% floor was not re-read.
- **CT — redemption:** "1 year" → "6 months (60 days if abandoned)". §12-157(f): redemption not later than six months after sale, or 60 days for abandoned property.
- **IA — bidMethod:** "Premium bid (highest dollar wins)" → "Bid-down ownership (smallest % of parcel wins)". Iowa Code §446.16(1): purchaser is the person who pays the total due for the smallest percentage of the parcel; minimum 1%.
- **IL — redemption:** "2–2.5 years" → "3 years (1 year for vacant, commercial, or 7+ unit property)". 35 ILCS 200/21-350 as amended by P.A. 104-553, eff. 7-10-26; applies to certificates issued on or after that date.
- **MA — rate:** "16%" → "8%". M.G.L. c.60 §62 now reads interest at 8 per cent upon the original sum for which the land was taken or sold.
- **MD — bidMethod:** "Bid-down interest rate (most counties)" → "Highest bidder (high-bid premium may apply)". Md. Tax-Prop. §14-817(a)(2), (b)(2): highest good faith bid; collector may require a high-bid premium.
- **NH — rate:** "18%" → "14%". RSA 80:32 and 80:69: interest at 14 percent per annum (amended 2018, 282, eff. April 1, 2019).
- **SD — rate:** "12%" → "10% max (bid down)". SDCL 10-23-8: no rate of interest higher than ten percent per year is a valid bid.

## Notes

- "Confirmed" means the official text read this run matched our data on the fields checked; the note says which fields were not re-read.
- Unconfirmed states kept their existing values; nothing was changed for them.
- Texas: statutes.capitol.texas.gov refused connections all run, so Texas stays unconfirmed.
- Illinois (build step): the site shows the redemption change as "2–2.5 years for older certificates; 3 years (1 year for vacant, commercial or 7+ unit property) for certificates from sales on or after July 10, 2026", because P.A. 104-553 applies only to certificates issued on or after its effective date.
- How changes reach the site: `cloudflare/scripts/build-site.mjs` applies each change to the state page and to every county record that still had the old state value. The legacy root data files were left alone (spec rule 5: don't touch the old app files). Each state page shows the old and new value.
