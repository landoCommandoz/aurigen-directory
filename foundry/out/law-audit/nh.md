# New Hampshire (NH) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/nh.json`.

Official code access: New Hampshire RSA chapter 80 (Collection of Taxes, including sections 80:19 to 80:42, 80:58 to 80:90), current text from gencourt.state.nh.us (New Hampshire General Court).

Finding: the site had an 18% rate, "Highest bidder (premium bid)" and municipality-held deeds. RSA 80:32 sets redemption interest at 14% per year; RSA 80:24 sells at auction for the percentage of the undivided interest a bidder will take for the unpaid tax, interest and costs; RSA 80:38 has the collector execute the deed to the purchaser after 2 years. Towns that adopt the lien procedure (RSA 80:58-86) allow only a municipality, county or the state to hold the lien (80:20-a, 80:63). Case-law claims (First NH Bank v. Windham, Polonsky v. Bedford) were removed; the statute (RSA 80:88-80:90) states the surplus and repurchase rules. The 10% penalty for repurchase replaces the "15%" figure.
