R.I. Gen. Laws § 44-9-22

§ 44-9-22. Proceedings as to low value lands unaffected by redemption provisions.

Nothing in §§ 44-9-19 - 44-9-21 nor in §§ 44-9-25 - 44-9-33 shall be construed to prevent the title of a person or a city or town purchasing
land at a sale under §§ 44-9-36 - 44-9-38 from becoming absolute without any foreclosure proceedings under these sections.

History of Section.
G.L. 1938, ch. 32, § 40; P.L. 1946, ch. 1800, § 1; G.L. 1956, § 44-9-22.
