Excess Proceeds

Excess proceeds from the sale of tax-foreclosed property are defined as any amount exceeding the total of unpaid and delinquent taxes, penalty, interest, and costs. Pursuant to AS 29.45.480, excess proceeds can be claimed by the former owner of the property. All claims must be in writing on a completed claim form received from this office and received no later than six months after the date of the tax foreclsoure auction. In the event the prior owner of record is deceased, a copy of the letters of administration required by AS 13.16.015 must accompany the claim form.

There are no list maintained for excess proceeds. All information regarding the tax foreclosure auction can be found on this webpage.
