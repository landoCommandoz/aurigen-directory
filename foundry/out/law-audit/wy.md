# Wyoming (WY) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/wy.json`.

Official code access: Wyoming Statutes Title 39 (full title PDF, including 39-13-108 (b) interest and (d) liens) from wyoleg.gov (Wyoming Legislature). The sections that set the county treasurer tax sale and certificate of purchase were not located in the text read (gap WY-1).

Finding: the site said Wyoming holds annual November tax lien sales, a fixed 15% rate and a 4-year redemption period. In the text of Title 39 read (W.S. 39-13-108): delinquent taxes bear interest at 18% per year until paid or collected (b)(ii); a holder of a certificate of purchase or tax deed has a lien that a district court can enforce by an order of sale (d)(ii); the sheriff sells at public auction, without appraisal, to the highest bidder for cash, and any person with an interest may redeem before the court confirms the sale (d)(iii); the sale balance goes to persons holding prior interests, claimable within two years of confirmation (d)(iv)(C). The 15% rate, November sales and 4-year redemption do not appear in the text read and were removed or sent to "Confirm with the county". The sections that set the county treasurer's tax sale and certificate of purchase were not located in the text read, so the actual sale process is a gap (WY-1).
