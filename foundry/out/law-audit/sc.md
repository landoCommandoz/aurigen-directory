# South Carolina (SC) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/sc.json`.

Official code access: South Carolina Code of Laws Title 12 Chapter 51 (sections 12-51-40 to 12-51-170, including 12-51-50, 12-51-60, 12-51-90 and 12-51-130) from scstatehouse.gov (South Carolina Legislature).

Finding: the site said South Carolina sells "tax lien certificates" at annual October to December sales, with a monthly "penalty" scale of 3% to 12%, a premium bid method, and cashier's-check rules. Title 12 chapter 51 says: real property is sold at public auction by the person charged with collecting delinquent taxes, on the advertised date, for payment in full by cash, cashier's check, certified check or money order (§12-51-50); the defaulting taxpayer, a grantee, or a mortgage or judgment creditor may redeem within twelve months (§12-51-90(A)); redemption interest is a lump sum of 3%, 6%, 9% or 12% of the bid amount by quarter, never more than the bid made for the forfeited land commission (§12-51-90(B)); if not redeemed, a tax title is made to the purchaser (§12-51-130); the remaining overage belongs to the owner of record before the end of the redemption period (§12-51-130). Lien-certificate, October to December, bid style, wire and inventory claims were corrected or removed.
