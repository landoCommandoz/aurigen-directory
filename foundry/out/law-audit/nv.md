# Nevada (NV) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/nv.json`.

Official code access: Nevada Revised Statutes chapter 361 (whole chapter, including 361.570, 361.585, 361.595, 361.600, 361.603 and 361.610) from leg.state.nv.us (Nevada Legislature).

Finding: the site said Nevada holds delinquent property 3 years, then sells it by "sealed bids followed by oral bids", with "no post-sale redemption" and immediate clean title. NRS chapter 361 says: the county treasurer holds the property in trust for 2 years after the first Monday in June of the certificate year, unless sooner redeemed (§361.570); persons listed in §361.585(4) can have it reconveyed by paying taxes, costs, penalties and interest, up to the third business day before a sale (§361.585(3)); the board of county commissioners may order the treasurer to sell for not less than the taxes, costs, penalties and interest, after notice of sale (§361.595); the buyer gets a quitclaim deed (§361.595(5)); suits to recover the land must be filed within 2 years after the deed is delivered (§361.600); excess-proceeds claims are due within 1 year after the deed is recorded (§361.610(5)). Sealed and oral bids appear in chapter 361 only for leases. The 3-year period, bid method, "no post-sale redemption", title-insurance and inventory claims were corrected or removed.
