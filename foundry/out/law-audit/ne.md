Official code access: Nebraska Revised Statutes sections 77-1801, 77-1817, 77-1818, 77-1823, 77-1824, 77-1827, 77-1830, 77-1831, 77-1837, 77-1844, 77-1845 and 45-104.01, from nebraskalegislature.gov (Nebraska Legislature). The page for 45-104.01 showed navigation only, so the interest rate itself was not read.

Finding: the site said Nebraska has a fixed 14% rate, a rotational bidding format, premium bids, a 3-year redemption, and over-the-counter sales after March. The statutes read say: delinquent real estate is subject to sale on or after the first Monday of March (§77-1801); the purchaser gets a perpetual lien (§77-1818); redemption interest is the §45-104.01 rate "as adjusted by the Legislature" from purchase to redemption, and the right to redeem ends when the purchaser applies for a tax deed (§77-1824); the purchaser applies within nine months after three years from the sale, after giving at least three months' notice (§77-1837, §77-1831). The 14% figure, rotational format, and bid method are not in the sections read and were removed or sent to "Confirm with the county". The §45-104.01 page showed navigation only, so the current rate is not stated (gap NE-1).
