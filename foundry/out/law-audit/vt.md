# Vermont (VT) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/vt.json`.

Official code access: Vermont Statutes Annotated Title 32 chapter 133 (full chapter, including sections 5252, 5254, 5255, 5259, 5260 and 5261) from legislature.vermont.gov (Vermont General Assembly).

Finding: the site called Vermont a "tax lien" state with a fixed 12% rate, "extremely rare" sales, 255 independent towns, and an overbid that is "NOT returned". 32 V.S.A. chapter 133 says: a town tax collector may extend a warrant on land when the taxpayer owes at least $1,500 and has been delinquent over a year, then advertise the land for public auction three weeks in a row (§5252); unpaid tax, costs and fees are satisfied by selling the real property (§5254); the owner, a lien holder or a mortgagee may redeem within one year from the day of sale by paying the sale sum plus interest at one percent per month or fraction thereof (§5260(a)); if not redeemed, the collector executes a deed to the purchaser (§5261). The chapter sections read do not say what happens to an overbid or excess proceeds, so that claim became "not stated, confirm" (gap VT-1). The lien, 12%, "rarely", 255-town and "not viable market" claims were corrected or removed.
