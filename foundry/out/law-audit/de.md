# Delaware (DE) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/de.json`.

Official code access: Delaware Code title 9, chapter 87, subchapters II (monition method, sections 8721 to 8728 and 8729) and IV (Kent and Sussex Counties, sections 8771 and 8776 and 8779), current text from delcode.delaware.gov (Delaware General Assembly). The chapter index page is UTF-16 and did not extract; the subchapter pages did.

Finding: the site called Delaware a "tax deed" state with a 15-20% penalty that varies by county, "premium bid (highest dollar)" and 60 days to 1 year by county. The Code (title 9, chapter 87) has two relevant procedures: subchapter II, the monition method (praecipe in Superior Court, writ of venditioni exponas to the sheriff, § 8729: redeem within 60 days of the Court's approval by paying the purchase price plus 15 percent in addition), and subchapter IV for Kent and Sussex Counties (§ 8776: no deed for 1 year; redeem at the purchase money plus 20 percent interest). A bid method (premium or highest dollar), the county count and the county websites are not in the statute and were removed. The statute does not say which subchapter each county uses beyond the Kent/Sussex heading of subchapter IV; the site does not say so either. Subchapter III (New Castle attachment) was listed but not read.
