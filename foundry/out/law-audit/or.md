# Oregon (OR) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/or.json`.

Official code access: Oregon Revised Statutes chapter 312 (2025 edition, including 312.010, 312.120, 312.200, 312.270, 312.520 to 312.560) from oregonlegislature.gov (Oregon Legislature).

Finding: the site said Oregon has "no post-sale redemption", opening bids at 80% of assessed value, a 3-year delinquency followed by a 2-year county hold before auction, and a "2025 ORS 312.510 update". ORS chapter 312 (2025 edition) says: property is subject to foreclosure after three years from the earliest date of delinquency (§312.010); the county holds it two years from the foreclosure judgment, during which interested persons may redeem by paying the judgment amount, interest, a 5% penalty and a fee (§312.120); unredeemed property is deeded to the county and redemption rights end (§312.200); the county may keep or transfer the property or sell it, by broker listing for some former primary residences or by public high-bid auction with a minimum starting bid of two-thirds of fair market value (§312.520); surplus claims go to the State Treasurer (§312.540). The 80% figure, the 2-year "hold before auction", "no post-sale redemption" (redemption actually runs before the county deed) and all opinion/margin claims were corrected or removed.
