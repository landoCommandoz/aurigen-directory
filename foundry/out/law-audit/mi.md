# Michigan (MI) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/mi.json`.

Official code access: Michigan Compiled Laws sections 211.78, 211.78k, 211.78m and 211.78t (General Property Tax Act), current text from legislature.mi.gov (Michigan Legislature).

Finding: the site said Michigan holds three annual auctions (July, September, and a November sale with "no minimum"), names Bid4Assets, GovEase and the Land Bank, quotes deposits of $500 to $2,000, and says Tyler "overhauled" the surplus law. The Act says: sales run from the third Tuesday in July to the first Tuesday in November after the foreclosure judgment (§211.78m(2)); property sells to the minimum bid or the highest amount above it; the minimum bid must include all delinquent taxes, interest, penalties and fees; there is no "no minimum" sale in the text read. Title vests in the foreclosing unit if taxes are not paid by March 31 after judgment (§211.78k(6)). Remaining-proceeds claims run through a Treasury form by July 1 and a circuit court motion (§211.78t). Section 211.78m(7) also covers property not previously sold (transfer to a city, land bank, etc.) and was not read in full; the site no longer says unsold property goes to a municipality (gap MI-1).

Lando decision A applied to platform, registration, deposit, dates, over-the-counter sales. Removed with no source: as-is and squatter claims, Wayne County Land Bank, drive-by photos, northern Michigan opportunity.
