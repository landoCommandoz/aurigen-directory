# Louisiana (LA) law audit

Checked 2026-10-27. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/la.json`.

Official code access: Louisiana Revised Statutes fetched from legis.la.gov (Louisiana State Legislature), print view.

## Finding

Louisiana's tax lien auction law (La. R.S. 47:2154, 47:2155, 47:2266.1) was rewritten by Acts 2024, No. 774 and Acts
2025, No. 411, both effective January 1, 2026. The current text confirms a bid-down monthly interest rate (maximum 1% a
month, no bid below 0.7% a month, lowest bid wins, noncompounding). The site's "3-year redemption" and "5% penalty" are
not in the sections read: the lien may be paid until 30 days after service of an enforcement petition, and the holder may
sue only after the later of three years from recording or six months after notices, within seven years of recording.
The site also named the parish sheriff; the statute assigns the auction to the tax collector.

## 2023 to 2026 enacted-law search

From the history notes of each fetched section on legis.la.gov (47:2154, 47:2155, 47:2241, 47:2266.1):
- Acts 2024, No. 774, §1 (effective Jan. 1, 2026): tax lien auction system.
- Acts 2025, No. 411, §1 (effective Jan. 1, 2026): further amendments to the same sections.
Both are in effect and reflected. Bill numbers were not looked up this run (the legislature's bill search was not run
for time). R.S. 47:2241.1 (redemption of pre-2026 tax sales under the law in effect on Dec. 31, 2025) and the 5% penalty
provision (R.S. 47:2127 or 47:2243) were not read; recheck in a later run.
