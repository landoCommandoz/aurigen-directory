# Missouri (MO) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/mo.json`.

Official code access: Revised Statutes of Missouri sections 140.150, 140.190, 140.230, 140.250 and 140.340, current text from revisor.mo.gov (Missouri Revisor of Statutes). Sections 140.260, 140.405, 140.420 and 140.980 were also downloaded but are not cited.

Finding: the site said Missouri has a flat 10% rate, "any bid accepted" at the third offering, no post-sale redemption, a St. Louis and Jackson County deed system with inverted lien priority, cashier's checks only, and that tax-lien sales are "not generally available" over the counter. The statutes read say: the sale is on the fourth Monday in August (§140.150); 10% is a ceiling on the interest rate specified in the certificate (§140.340.2); redemption is an absolute right for one year then defeasible until the purchaser takes the deed (§140.340.1); at the third offering the highest bid may not be below taxes, interest, penalties and costs and a 90-day redemption applies, and later sales carry no redemption (§140.250); nonresidents cannot bid and must use a county-citizen agent (§140.190); surplus goes to former lienholders then owners (§140.230). The St. Louis and Jackson inverted-priority claim, IRS lien claim and rural-county advice were not found in the sections read and were removed.

Sections 140.260, 140.405, 140.420 and 140.980 were downloaded but not used for site statements (gap MO-1). Lando decision A applied to place, registration, payment method.
