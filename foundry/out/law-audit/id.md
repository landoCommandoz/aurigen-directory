# Idaho (ID) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/id.json`.

Official code access: Idaho Statutes, title 63 chapter 10 (sections 63-1001, 63-1003, 63-1005, 63-1007) and title 31 section 31-808, from legislature.idaho.gov (Idaho Legislature). The site says statutes are updated each July 1 following the session.

Finding: the site said Idaho counties may or may not sell, "some haven't held sales in years", no statutory rate, and no post-sale redemption. The Code says: delinquent taxes bear 1% per month (§63-1001); a delinquency not redeemed within 3 years leads to a tax deed to the county (§63-1005); the right of redemption runs 14 months from that deed unless the county has contracted to sell or deeded the property (§63-1007); and for tax deeded property the board of county commissioners "shall conduct an auction no later than fourteen (14) months" from the deed, selling to the highest bidder with a board-set minimum bid (§31-808(1)). The "counties have discretion whether to sell" claim is contradicted by that sentence and was removed. §31-808(2) also sets surplus handling for tax deeded property (pass 2: shown text trimmed to the apportionment clause the quote contains; the 60-day claim period in (2)(c) is not on the site yet).

Lando decision A applied: place, registration, deposit and dates now say "Confirm with the county". Removed with no official source: housing-shortage demand, Boise competition, rural parcel issues, calling Ada and Canyon first, no central calendar.
