# Virginia (VA) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/va.json`.

Official code access: Code of Virginia §§ 58.1-3965, 58.1-3967, 58.1-3968, 58.1-3969, 58.1-3970, 58.1-3971, 58.1-3972, 58.1-3974 and 58.1-3975 (Title 58.1, chapter 39, article 4) from law.lis.virginia.gov (Virginia Legislative Information System, Virginia General Assembly), each page dated 11/1/2026.

Finding: the site said Virginia requires 3 years of delinquency, has "no post-sale redemption", sells by oral upset bids, and puts attorney costs at $1,500 to $3,500. Title 58.1 chapter 39 article 4 says: real estate with delinquent taxes may be sold to collect them, after taxes are delinquent on December 31 following the second anniversary of their due date (shorter for some condemned, nuisance, derelict or blighted property) (§58.1-3965(A)); the proceeding is a complaint in circuit court (§58.1-3967); the court may appoint a special commissioner to sell (§58.1-3969); the owner may redeem before the date set for the judicial sale (§58.1-3974); the former owner is entitled to the surplus over taxes, penalties, interest, fees, costs and liens (§58.1-3967). The 3-year period, "no post-sale redemption", oral upset bids, attorney costs, NoVA and county inventory claims were corrected or removed.
