# Ohio (OH) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/oh.json`.

Official code access: Ohio Revised Code chapter 5721 (sections 5721.01 to 5721.46, including 5721.19, 5721.20, 5721.30, 5721.32, 5721.33, 5721.38 and 5721.41) from codes.ohio.gov (Ohio Legislative Service Commission).

Finding: the site said Ohio is a "hybrid" with "34+" lien counties, a 6% rate floor, a 1-year redemption period, SRI as the platform, and "upset bids". ORC chapter 5721 says: at a tax certificate auction the county treasurer or designee starts bidding at 18% simple interest per year and accepts lower bids in steps of one-fourth of one per cent down to 0%, and the lowest rate wins (§5721.32(C)); the treasurer may set a certificate period of three to six years (§5721.32(A)); six per cent of the purchase price is a minimum part of the redemption price, not a bidding floor (§5721.30(E)); bidders register with the treasurer and pay a $500 cash fee, and the winning bidder pays at least a 10% cash deposit that day and the balance within five business days (§5721.32(B), (D)); the owner or others entitled may redeem by paying the redemption prices (§5721.38); a foreclosed parcel may be sold without appraisal for not less than fair market value plus costs or the total finding plus costs (§5721.19(A)); sale balances go to the clerk of court within 45 days after confirmation (§5721.20(A)). "34+ counties", the 6% floor, the 1-year redemption, SRI and county-specific claims were corrected or removed.
