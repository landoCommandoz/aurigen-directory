# Maryland (MD) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/md.json`.

Official code access: Maryland Code, Tax-Property Article, Title 14, Subtitle 8, sections 14-808, 14-817, 14-818, 14-820, 14-825, 14-827, 14-833, 14-836, 14-844 and 14-847, current text from mgaleg.maryland.gov (Maryland General Assembly, Department of Legislative Services statute text).

Finding: the site said rates run 6% to 24% (Montgomery/PG 20%, Anne Arundel 18%, Baltimore County 12%), bidding was "bid-down interest rate", and the 9-month foreclosure wait applied only in Baltimore City. Section 14-820 sets the rate of redemption at 6% a year, with some counties' base figures at 10% (Calvert, Caroline, Dorchester, Garrett) or 14% (Carroll) "or as fixed by" the county; owner-occupied residential property may not exceed 10%. § 14-817 sells to the highest good faith accepted bid, with an optional high-bid premium. § 14-833: 6 months (9 months for owner-occupied residential) before a foreclosure complaint, certificate void after 2 years. County-fixed rates (20%, 18%, 12%) and Bid4Assets/RealAuction were not in the code and were replaced by the statutory figures or "Confirm with the county".
