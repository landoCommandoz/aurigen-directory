Finding: the site said Rhode Island's redemption penalty is 16% for six months then 1% per month, with 39 irregular municipal "lien sales", a 1-year redemption and an East Providence sale confirmed for April 15, 2026. R.I. Gen. Laws chapter 44-9 says: the collector sells at public auction after notice posted at least three weeks before (§44-9-9); the collector's deed is subject to the right of redemption and held as security (§44-9-12); the penalty is 10% of the purchase price if redeemed within six months, plus 1% more for each later month (§44-9-19(a)); redemption stays open until a foreclosure petition is filed, which may be after one year from the sale, and a decree then forever bars redemption (§44-9-25, §44-9-30). The 16% figure, the "39 municipalities", volume and platform claims, and the East Providence date (no official source read) were corrected or removed. Section 44-9-7 returned no statute text, so the advertising and taking rules in it were not read.
