# Montana (MT) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/mt.json`.

Official code access: Montana Code Annotated 2025: sections 15-17-125, 15-17-323, 15-17-322, 15-18-111, 15-18-112, 15-18-114 and 15-16-102, plus the section indexes for Title 15 chapter 17 parts 1 to 3 and chapter 18 part 1, from mca.legmt.gov (Montana Legislature).

Finding: the site said Montana "assigns tax liens annually in July", with a "10% + 2% per month" rate, a "fixed rate - no bidding", a premium bid method, and cited MCA §15-17-112 and §15-18-111. MCA 2025 says: the county treasurer attaches a tax lien no later than the first working day in August (§15-17-125); the county must assign a county-held lien to a person who gives proof of mail notice and pays the delinquent taxes, penalties, interest and costs, and the treasurer sets a policy when several people seek the same assignment (§15-17-323); redemption is by the first working day in August, 3 years after attachment (2 years for certain vacant special-improvement-district lots) (§15-18-111); delinquent taxes draw interest at 5/6 of 1% a month and a 2% penalty (§15-16-102); an assignee is repaid the amount paid plus interest as specified there (§15-18-114). §15-17-112, the old tax lien sale sections (15-17-201 to 214) and others are repealed. The July date, 10%/2% per month, bid method, inventory and platform claims were corrected or removed. Note: the 10% per year figure is not stated in the statute; only 5/6 of 1% a month is shown.
