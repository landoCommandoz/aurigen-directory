Enacted law found: P.L.2024, c.39, effective immediately (July 10, 2024). It lets an owner demand a judicial sale or Internet auction run by the county sheriff before final judgment (R.S. 54:5-87(b)). The site's "strict foreclosure, no public sale" claim was corrected. It also moves the premium handling (R.S. 54:5-33) and extends the five years during bankruptcy.
