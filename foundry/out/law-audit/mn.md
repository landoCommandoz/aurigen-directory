# Minnesota (MN) law audit

Checked 2026-11-01. Status: done. Ledger: `cloudflare/data/law/mn.json`.

Official code access: Minnesota Statutes sections 280.01, 282.01, 282.08 (with the Revisor's note) and 282.241 (2026 Statutes), from revisor.mn.gov (Office of the Revisor of Statutes). Sections 279.37, 281.17, 281.18, 281.23, 282.016 and 282.018 were also downloaded but are not cited.

Finding: the site said Minnesota has "no post-sale redemption", "minimum bids at assessed value", a 7% interest rate for 2026, county land banks and a Tyler surplus-return requirement. The statutes read say: on the second Monday in May the county auditor sells judgment parcels and bids them in for the state (§280.01); forfeited land may not be sold for less than the appraised value (§282.01, with exceptions in subdivision 7a); and the former owner may repurchase forfeited land before the state sells it (§282.241). The 7% rate, land banks and "assessed value" are not in the sections read and were removed or corrected. The Revisor's note to §282.08 says the excess-proceeds apportionment was found a taking in Tyler, but the section text is unchanged: surplus.1 is flagged (see decisions-needed.md).

Sections 279.37, 281.17, 281.18, 281.23, 282.016 and 282.018 were downloaded but not read closely, so interest and redemption of unforfeited parcels (chapter 281) are not stated on the site (gap MN-1).
