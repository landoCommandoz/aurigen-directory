Update on this post.

A lot of you asked where to find the actual rules for each state. I have been checking them against the official text, state by state, and I put the result in one place.

What it is: the Platbook Rules Sheet. For each of 44 states it lists how the tax sale works (sale type, the interest or penalty rate set by law, redemption, bid method, where and when the sale is held, how to register and pay, buying after the sale, money left after the sale, and what to check before you bid). Every line shows the statute, a link to the official page, the date I checked it, and the exact words it rests on.

What it is not: legal advice, a list of properties, or a county-by-county list. Counties can add their own rules, so confirm dates and rules with the county before you bid.

How honest I can be about its limits: 7 states are marked "checked". 37 are marked "checked with gaps", and the gaps are listed in plain words on each state. Anything I could not confirm from an official source is left out, not guessed. Seven states are not in it yet.

Where the sources come from: state legislature code sites and enacted acts, state revenue and land office pages, and a county treasurer's own page when a detail only exists at county level.

It is free in exchange for an email address, and the key works for 12 months while more states are added. There is a sample with two full states on the page, so you can judge it before you hand over the address. Nothing is charged, so there is nothing to refund.
