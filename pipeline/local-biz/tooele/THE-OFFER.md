# THE OFFER: WHY WE'RE DIFFERENT
There are a lot of people selling "$0 down websites." This is what makes ours
not even the same product. Brian and Marty should know this list cold.

## What every client gets (rattle these off on the phone)

1. **The site already exists before we ever call.** Built from their real Google
   reviews, their real photos, their real hours. Everyone else sells a promise.
   We text a finished link.
2. **English AND Spanish.** Two full versions of the site. Nobody doing cheap
   local sites offers this, and in Tooele it matters for real customers.
3. **A short intro video on the site.** A few seconds of motion at the top makes
   a $99 site look like a $5,000 site. No competitor at this price has video.
4. **Google Business listing tune-up.** Hours, photos, description, website link
   all squared away. Agencies charge hundreds for this alone. We fold it in.
5. **A review card for the counter.** Printed card with a QR code straight to
   their Google review page. More reviews means more customers, and they think
   of us every time one comes in.
6. **Text-message service.** Want something changed? Text us, done within a day.
   No ticket systems, no logins, no "portal."
7. **Local and in person.** We live here. If an owner wants to see it face to
   face, one of us walks in with a phone. No agency does that in Tooele.
8. **No contract, and a real guarantee.** Cancel by text. If the site is ever
   down more than a day, that month is free.

## What the money covers

**$149 one-time setup:** their own dot-com domain, the Google listing work,
the Spanish version, the intro video, the review card. Companies charge $500
to $2,500 for this bundle. We charge $149 because the monthly relationship is
the business, not the up-front payday.

**$99 a month:** hosting, keeping it live and secure, unlimited small edits by
text, and a once-a-month plain-English note on how the site is doing.
