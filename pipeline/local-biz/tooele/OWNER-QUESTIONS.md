# WHEN THE OWNER HAS QUESTIONS
Answers for Brian and Marty. Read this twice before the first call.
Don't recite it word for word on the phone. Know the answers, then just talk.

## BEFORE ANYBODY DIALS (do these first)

1. **Pick a name.** When an owner asks "what company is this?" you need an answer.
   Even "BM Web Services" or "Tooele Web Co" works. Decide it once, both of you use it.
2. **Decide how you take payment.** Easiest: a Stripe payment link set to $99/month
   subscription, texted right after they say yes. Venmo works for the first ones
   but a card on file is what makes the monthly actually show up.
3. **Get Surge set up** (free hosting) so demo sites can go live fast after a yes.
4. **Split the list.** One of you takes odd numbers, the other takes evens, or
   split by tier. Don't both call the same business.
5. **Keep a call log.** Date, business, who answered, what they said, callback time.
   A notebook is fine. Half this game is calling back when you said you would.

## THE MONEY QUESTIONS

**"How much is it?"**
"Nothing to build it and nothing to look at it. If you want to keep it,
it's 149 one-time to get you set up proper and 99 a month after. The
monthly covers hosting, any changes you want, and me keeping it running."

**"What's the 149 for?"**
"That's the real work up front. Your own dot-com address, getting your
Google listing squared away, a Spanish version of the site, and the intro
video. Web companies charge 500 to 2,500 bucks for that part alone. I keep
it at 149 because I'd rather have you as a monthly client than hit you
with a big bill."

**"What do I get for 99 a month?"**
"The site stays live, I handle all the tech stuff, and when you want something
changed, new photos, new prices, new hours, you text me and I do it. You never
touch anything."

**"That's expensive."**
"A web design company charges two to five grand up front for less than this,
plus monthly on top. My whole setup is 149. And honestly, one job from a
customer who found you online covers a couple months of it."
(For trades this is literally true. One plumbing call runs $150 to $400.)

**"Is there a contract?"**
"No contract. Month to month. If you want out, text me and it's done."

**"Can I just pay once and own it?"**
"The 99 is what keeps it hosted, updated, and working. If you'd rather pay
one price and run it yourself we can talk about that, but most guys don't
want to deal with it. That's the point of the monthly."
(If they push hard: a one-time build is a real option. Quote $500 to $1,000
one-time, no monthly, they handle their own hosting after. Better to keep
them monthly.)

**"What happens if I cancel?"**
"The site comes down. No fees, no fight. If you bought your own domain name,
that's yours and you keep it."

## THE TRUST QUESTIONS

**"Who is this? What company are you with?"**
"[Your business name]. I'm local, out here in Tooele. It's a small operation,
which is why I can do it at this price."

**"How did you get my number?"**
"Same place your customers would look. Your Google listing. It's public."

**"Is this a scam?"**
"Fair question, you get a lot of junk calls. Here's the thing though. You don't
pay me anything today. I build the site, you look at it, and only if you like
it do we talk money. There's no version of this where you're out anything."

**"Can I see other sites you've made?"**
If you have live demos: "Yeah, I'll text you a couple links with yours."
If you don't yet: "I build each one custom from your actual reviews and photos,
so the best example I can show you is yours. Give me a day and you'll see it."

**"Let me think about it." / "Call me back."**
"Sure. When's good, tomorrow or the day after?" Get a specific time. Write it
down. Call back exactly then. That callback is where half the closes happen.

**"My nephew does websites." / "I know a guy."**
"That's great, and if he ever builds it, cancel on me anytime. But right now
you've got nothing, and I can have you live this week."

## THE TECHNICAL QUESTIONS (plain answers, don't get technical)

**"I already have a Facebook page."**
"Facebook's good, keep it. But when somebody googles 'plumber Tooele' your
Facebook page isn't what comes up. The site shows up on Google and links
right to your Facebook. They work together."

**"Will I show up on Google?"**
"The site gets you into Google results and ties into your Google business
listing. It's not instant, takes a few weeks to settle in, and nobody honest
can promise you the number one spot. What I can promise is that right now
people literally cannot find a website for you, and after this they can."
(NEVER promise rankings or "first page." That's the fastest way to a refund
fight later.)

**"What's the web address going to be?"**
"It starts on a free address so you can see it. If you keep it, we get you a
real dot-com, usually about 12 bucks a year, and I set all that up."

**"Can I make changes myself?"**
"You just text me what you want changed and I do it. Most owners like that
better than logging into anything."

**"Where do the photos and reviews come from?"**
"Your Google listing. That's what I build from. If you've got better photos
on your phone, text them to me and I'll swap them in."

**"Can it take bookings / orders?"**
"I can link it to whatever you already use, Square, Booksy, whatever. Buttons
right on the site."

**"Can I get an email like info@mybusiness.com?"**
"Once you've got your own domain, yeah, that can be set up. Let's get the
site live first."

**"What if the site goes down?"**
"That's my job. That's part of what the monthly is. You don't watch it, I do."

## CALL MECHANICS FOR FIRST-TIMERS

- **Gatekeeper answers** (employee, spouse): "Is the owner around? What time's
  he usually in? What's his name?" Write it down, call back, ask for him by name.
- **Voicemail:** "Hey, this is [name], local out here in Tooele. Real quick, I
  noticed you don't have a website and that's what I do. Nothing to buy today,
  I just want to show you something. Call or text me at [number]." Under 20
  seconds. Then try again in two days at a different hour.
- **They're busy / mid-job:** "When's a better time, tonight or tomorrow morning?"
  Specific choices get real answers.
- **They get short with you:** Stay friendly, get out clean. "All good, have a
  good one." Small town. You'll see these people at the gas station.
- **Nerves:** Totally normal. The first five calls are practice, start with
  Tier 3 numbers 24 to 29 so the misses don't cost you the best leads. By call
  ten it's routine.
- **The whole job on the phone is one thing:** get permission to text the link.
  Not to close the sale. The site does the selling. Get the yes, get the best
  text number, get off the phone.
