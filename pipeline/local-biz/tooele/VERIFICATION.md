## What couldn't be checked

Yelp and some directories block direct page loads, so a few website fields were read
from search results instead of the live page. Facebook page link fields mostly can't
be opened by our tools. Both limits are flagged per business in the research logs.
The safety net: every opener asks, it never assumes. If an owner says "we have a
website," the caller says "must have missed it, where is it?" and moves to the
upgrade angle or politely out.
