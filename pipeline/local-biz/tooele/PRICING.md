# PRICE POINT: WHY $99 A MONTH IS RIGHT
Researched 2026-08-08. Every number below came from published pricing pages.

## The short answer
$99 a month, free build, no contract. That's the answer. It sits dead center of
what real companies charge for this exact model, and it's easy to defend on the
phone with one sentence: "one job from the site covers three months."

## What web design companies charge (one-time builds)
- Utah agencies: $1,000 to $2,500 for a small site, $2,500 to $7,500 typical
- Published Utah examples: PageForge $1,500-$2,500, Utah Website Design $2,500-$4,000, Spigot Design $5,000-$10,000
- Salt Lake agencies run $1,500 to $10,000 and up
- And after the build they still charge $50 to $200 a month for maintenance
- Utah maintenance-only plans are published at $75 to $99 a month, with no build included

**What this means on the phone:** we charge less per month than agencies charge
just to babysit a site the owner already paid thousands for.

## Freelancers
- $500 to $5,000 one-time for a small business site, $1,000 to $4,000 typical for 5 pages
- Hourly rates $50 to $100

## The pay-monthly companies (our exact model: $0 down)
- Redefine Web: $99/mo for their base plan, hosting and edits included
- GoHighLevel resellers: $99/mo with a free build for local service businesses
- Hello Web Designs: from $150/mo, custom coded
- Jottful: from $65/mo plus a build fee up front
- RateGather: $33/mo at the bottom of the market, template work
- The going rate for a real, edits-included, $0-down offer is **$99 to $150 a month**

## DIY builders (what the owner would do alone)
- Wix $17 to $159/mo, Squarespace from $19/mo, GoDaddy $10 to $21/mo
- Cheaper on paper, but the owner does everything: design, writing, photos,
  updates, fixing it when it breaks. What we sell is "you never touch it."

## The math that closes trades
- Average plumbing job: about $315 (bigger repairs $500 to $800)
- Average HVAC repair: about $350
- Average auto repair order: $500 to $749
- Average electrician ticket: $348 to $419

One average job pays for 3 to 6 months of the site. Say it exactly like that.

## The setup fee
A one-time setup fee is normal in this market and $149 is on the friendly end:
Jottful charges $99 to $499 up front for the build, agencies charge $1,000 and
up before a site exists at all. Keep the fee justified by real deliverables
(domain, Google listing work, Spanish version, intro video) so it never feels
like a junk fee. It also puts cash in the closers' pockets the same week,
which keeps the calls happening. Mention it at the close, never in the opener.
The hook number is always "99 a month."

## When to use a different number
- **$79? No.** Utah maintenance-only plans already cost that and include no build.
  Going lower just makes it look cheap.
- **$149 later.** Once there are live client sites to show and extras to bundle
  (Google Business profile management, review requests), $149 is defensible.
  Raise it for new clients only, never on existing ones.
- **One-time fallback:** if an owner flat refuses monthly, $500 to $1,000
  one-time, they own it, no support after. Use it to save a dying call, not
  as the lead offer. Monthly is the business.
